Before You Begin...

If you choose to double-check the time totals, then be advised that adding hours and minutes requires special conversion. For example,  00:30:00  hours (that is, 30 minutes) must be entered as  0.5   and not as  0.3  .

Optionally, there are websites that can easily add time for you.
