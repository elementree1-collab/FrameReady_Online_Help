Outbound Consignments Form View

Consignments is used to track products that are going out of your physical store/warehouse and is designed to only work with items in the Products file.

Tip: The Outbound Consignments file is designed similar to the Invoice file.

Outbound Consignments Form View Explained

Printer icon Button

  • Prints the current record.

New Consignment button

  • Creates a blank record and switches to the data entry screen.

Customer Information Section

  • Changes made here will be reflected in the Contacts file.

Not Tax Exempt Fields and Checkboxes

  • Auto-filled from the Contacts file if certificate numbers have been entered. These check boxes may be over-ridden.

Consignment # Field

  • Automatically generated number, non-modifiable.

Date Field

  • Date assigned to the current record. Click to change or set to today's date.

Consignment Details Section

PO# Field

  • Record a purchase order number.

Terms Dropdown Field

  • Record the terms governing this consignment. Choose from an editable drop-down list.

Entered By Dropdown Field

  • Record the employee who filled out this record.

Due Date Field

  • Record when the consignment is due back.

Ship Via Dropdown Field

  • Record the means of transportation, e.g. company truck, courier company, etc.

F.O.B.

  • Free On Board, an historical trade term requiring the seller to deliver goods on board a vessel designated by the buyer. The seller fulfills its obligations to deliver when the goods have passed over the ship's rail.

Line Entry Section

  • Items assigned to this Consignment Order are listed here.

  • Line item entries are locked and cannot be modified except via the Data Entry button.

Data Entry Button

  • Use to add or change Line Item entries for the current record.

  • If a Consignment Order has been marked Processed, it cannot be modified using the Data Entry button. You can, however, check items in.

  • See also: Outbound Consignment Data Entry Screen

Subtotal, Tax1, Tax2 and Total Fields

  • These are non-modifiable fields.

Relookup Prices Button

Shipping Information Section

Tip: If the destination is different than the address of the consignee as shown at the top of the screen, then record the address in Ship To.

This field will auto-fill if the Shipping tab is used in the Contacts file, however data entered here will not be recorded in your Contacts.
