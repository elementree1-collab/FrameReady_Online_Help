Here are some non-obvious features in FrameReady that may be time-saving short-cuts for you.

Clever FrameReady Secrets

In the Work Orders File...

Total Field

  • Click the dollar value to see a Price Map of the current job.

  • See: Price Map Explained

Number of Pieces Due Button

  • Click the button below the Due Date to see all the Work Orders due on the specified date. The text in the button tells you how many; if blank then no Work Orders are due on that date.

Sub Total Button (Calculator Icon)

  • Calculates the total of all the Work Orders for the current customer for this session or for Work Orders with the same Job # in the Shop Tab.

  • Useful when a customer has several pieces to be framed and wants to know the subtotal after two or three Work Orders. Clicking on this button  creates a found set.

In the Price Codes File...

For any Moulding Record

  • Click the Retail Price Per Foot field to see the Formula used to price the item.

In the Invoices File...

Total Due Field

  • Click the dollar value (shown here in red) and FrameReady finds and shows you all the outstanding Invoices belonging to the current customer.

In the Contacts File...

Send Button

  • If you perform a search for users with an email address, you can send a single email to all records in the found set (addresses are added to the BCC: field). Unless you limit the found set to a few dozen, you may be adversely flagged as a spammer.
