Take a Retail Product Inventory Count

Main Menu > Price Codes section > Find Price Code Item button

How to edit a Work Order when the Auto Update of materials inventory feature is active.

  • The How to Print Retail Product Summary can be printed in four different sort orders; by Artist, Category, Medium or Supplier. Total quantity in stock and wholesale value will appear at the bottom of the last page. There is also a Retail Product Detail Report.

  • Top selling items can be set for automatic reordering when falling below prescribed thresholds.

  • Year end inventory reports for physical counting may be printed at the end of the fiscal year. See Steps to Closing Off my Year End.

How to take a Retail Product Inventory Count

  1. Go to Products file.

  2. Click the Find All button and then the List View button.

    OR

    You may want to search for only those items that are in stock, i.e. click Find and enter  >0  into the Qty field.

  3. In list view, click the Inventory List View button.



    The Inventory List View screen appears.

  4. Optionally, use the Sort button to sort the items by location or quantity or whatever will help you in your manual inventory.

  5. Optionally, click the Print Inventory Report button to print a document to use in conjunction with your physical inventory count.

    Caution: This may consume many sheets of printer paper.

  6. In the Inventory List screen, for each item, enter the amounts which have been counted into the Actual field.



    When you entered a number into the Actual field, the difference is immediately calculated and put into the Variance field. This will provide you with a variance report of what you thought you had in inventory and what is actually there. Large variances should be investigated.

  7. When you enter zero  0  into an Actual field, then, during step #10,  the value in the Quantity field will be replaced with a zero.

  8. After you have entered your counts into the Actual column, then you can move all of the numbers into the Qty field.

    To move the numbers from the Actual field to the Qty field, go to the Main Menu, in the Products section and click the Clear Inventory Fields button. 



    The Clear Inventory Fields window appears.

  9. Click the button titled, Move the values from the Actual field to Quantity field and clear the Actual field.



    You will not be asked to confirm this action.

  10. Do NOT click the Clear All Price Code Items button as this will delete the amount in ALL of the Quantity fields.

  11. When finished, the Main Menu appears.

Why is there a negative quantity on my physical inventory list?

  • A negative quantity appears when a retail item has been sold but not first recorded as received into inventory.
