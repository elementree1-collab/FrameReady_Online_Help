Edit the Gallery Consignment Agreement

Main Menu > Products

How to Edit the Gallery Consignment Agreement

  1. Make sure you are open in level4.

  2. On Main Menu, in the menu bar, click Perform and choose Restore Full Menus.

  3. Go to Products file.

  4. In the menu bar, click View and choose Status Toolbar.

  5. Again in the menu bar, click View and choose Layout Mode.

  6. In top left corner, in the Layout field, click on Form View.

  7. Scroll down the list (near the bottom) and choose Gallery Consignment Agreement.

  8. In the toolbar, click on the  T  icon in order to be able to type any changes to the text.

  9. Do not change any wording between arrows, as these are auto-fill merge fields. Eg. <<MS::YourBusinessName> > as these are auto-fill fields.

  10. Make your text changes.

  11. To see how the changes will look: in the menu bar, click View and choose Preview Mode.

  12. You are asked if you want to save your changes. If you are uncertain about what you have done or do not want to save the changes you just made, then click Don’t Save.

  13. To continue making changes: in the menu bar, click View and choose Layout Mode again.

  14. Once you are finished making your changes, then click View and choose Form View.

  15. In the Layout field, click Gallery Consignment Agreement.

  16. Scroll up the list to select Form View.

  17. You will be asked if you want to save your changes. If you are uncertain about what you have done or do not want to save the changes you just made, then click Don’t Save. Do not check the Save layout changes automatically checkbox.

  18. In the menu bar, click View and choose Browse Mode to return to regular browsing mode.

  19. In the menu bar, click View and choose Status Toolbar to hide the toolbar.
