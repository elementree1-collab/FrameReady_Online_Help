Web Portal Invoices

  • After the customer clicks the Invoices button, a List View of all their invoices in FrameReady appears.

  • This view shows basic information about the invoices; such as the invoice number, totals, payments, status and more.

  • If the customer clicks an Invoice number, a detail view for that particular invoice appears. They can select or add shipping addresses as well as make credit card payments on that invoice.

Invoices Detail View

  • On the Invoices Detail screen, almost all of the information about the invoice (that you can also see in FrameReady) appears, including:

    • Customer Information

    • Shipping Address

    • Invoice Details

    • Line Items

    • Totals and Payments

  • Customers can click the Edit Shipping Address button next to the Shipping Address to edit the shipping address on the invoice.

  • After clicking the Edit Shipping Address button, all of the shipping addresses that the customer has added for their Contact account appear.

  • The customer can click on the + button (left of each shipping address) to select that particular shipping address to add.

  • Customers can also add a new shipping address by clicking the + Add Shipping Address button and entering their new shipping address details. It will then connect the new shipping address to the invoice and also add the new shipping address to their Contact record in the FrameReady.

Processing a Payment on an Invoice in the Web Portal

  • Customers can process a credit card payment on their Invoice directly in the Web Portal by clicking the + New Payment button (bottom right of the Invoice details screen in the Payments portal) and selecting the type of credit card they are using, the date of the payment that they would like to post the payment for and the amount of the payment.



       

  • After entering the information for their payment, the customer must click the Process CC Transaction button to go to the next screen where they must enter the card information for the credit card they are using to process the payment.

  • The customer must enter the full card number, the expiration date, the CVV on the back of the card, and the billing address on the card. After this, the customer must click the Pay (amount) button to begin the payment processing.

  • An animation plays on the screen while the payment is processing. After the payment is complete, the customer will see a checkmark on the animation letting them know that the payment has been successfully processed.

  • If the payment was rejected for any reason, the user will see a red X on the animation letting them know it was unsuccessful. If the payment was unsuccessful, the customer must click the < Go Back button to re-enter the payment details and attempt to process the payment again.

  • If the payment was successfully completed, then the customer can click the Click to continue after payment has been processed button. This will record the details of the payment into FrameReady and apply the payment to the invoice.




  • After the payment has been applied to the Invoice in FrameReady, the Web Portal directs the customer back to the Invoice detail screen where they can see the new payment that has been applied in the Payments portal near the bottom right of the screen.

  • They can also see the date, tender, amount of the payment and the FrameReady Pay powered by Fortis reference number for the payment.

  • This reference number is the same reference number that you can look up in your Payment Logistics / Fortis web portal to directly reference the transaction. This same reference number will also appear in FrameReady on the invoice to your FrameReady users so they will be able to verify.
