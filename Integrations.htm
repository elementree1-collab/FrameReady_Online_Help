FrameReady Integrations

Main Menu > Setup Data > Integrations tab

Expand what FrameReady can do by connecting it to other software and online services.

  • A FrameReady Integration is when you connect FrameReady to different software programs and online websites.

  • Once connected and set up, our FrameReady integrations let you share information, such as sending invoices to QuickBooks Online, talking to CMC software, sending messages from FrameReady, recording Square payments in FrameReady, and more.

Available Integrations

You can connect FrameReady to the following software and online services.

Square Payments

  • FrameReady is integrated with Square and offers you the option to process credit card transactions, refunds, and voids using your Square account and your Square Terminal.

  • Square Overview

Payment Logistics Credit Card Processing

  • FrameReady is integrated with Payment Logistics' credit card processing and offers you the option to complete your transactions within FrameReady and without the need for an external credit card processor. US Only.

  • Credit Card Processing Overview

QuickBooks Online

  • FrameReady is integrated with QuickBooks Online and offers you the option to push invoices, payments (including taxes), and customer information to your QuickBooks Online account.

  • QuickBooks Overview

Constant Contact

  • Import your Constant Contact Lists into FrameReady and then assign or remove Contacts to any of your email marketing campaigns.

  • Constant Contact Overview

MailChimp

  • Import your MailChimp Audiences and subscribe/unsubscribe individual Contacts to any of your email marketing campaigns.

  • Mailchimp Overview

SMS Text Messaging

  • FrameReady is integrated with third-party SMS services BulkSMS and ClickSend for sending text notifications to your customers.

  • Send SMS Messages to Contacts

Gunnar CMC

  • FrameReady is integrated with the Gunnar Mat Designer software and offers you the option to select and build Gunnar designs.

  • CMC Gunnar

Wizard CMC with Visualizations

  • FrameReady is integrated with the Mat Designer program FrameShop by Wizard, which offers you the option to connect Work Orders to FrameShop and vice versa.

  • CMC Wizard FrameShop

Valiani CMC

  • FrameReady is integrated with the Valiani V Studio software "Create" and "Smart Cut", which offers you the option to create a Valiani template, attach it to a Work Order, and push the Work Order directly into the Valiani software.

  • CMC Valiani

Also Available

The following non-integrations are also available when using FrameReady.

FileMaker Server

  • FrameReady can be used on single computer or in a network environment. The requirements are different for each of these cases. FileMaker Server is required to use FrameReady on multiple computers and devices on your network.

  • FileMaker Server Overview

Digital Photos

  • Having a digital image of a moulding profile or a piece of consignment art can be invaluable as a reference. Photos can be stored in different areas in FrameReady: retail products, customer's artwork, moulding profiles.

  • Digital Images

Barcode Scanning

  • Scan moulding and matboard barcodes quickly into Work Orders, as well as print barcode labels. Requires a barcode reader; models available online.

  • Barcodes Overview

Send Emails

  • FrameReady has built-in barcode support that offers you quick scanning of moulding and matboard barcodes into Work Orders.

  • Email Correspondence
