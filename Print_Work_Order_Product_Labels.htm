Print Work Order Product Labels

Main Menu > Work Orders > Print Documents button

When a Work Order includes the purchase of artwork or other materials, you may need a label for that specific product to identify it for your production team. Using this proceedure will create and print the label directly from the Work Order file.

(Sample Label)

  • These labels are built into all copies of FrameReady.

  • Review the Labels Overview to learn more about labels and editing them to suit your business's needs.

Printing Product Labels from a Work Order

  1. On a Work Order that includes a Product item, click the Print Documents sidebar button.

  2. Click the Product Labels button.

  3. If you have selected to see a print preview in your Work Order Defaults, then click Continue to move to the Print Options Menu to select your printer.

Printing Product Labels - Menubar

  1. Select Print on the Menubar at the top of the screen.

  2. Select Product Label from the list of options on the pull down list.

  3. If you have selected to see a print preview in your Work Order Defaults, then click Continue to move to the Print Options Menu to select your printer.
