Orders have the Wrong Vendor

The Problem

  • Instead of the expected vendor appearing on a Frame or Purchase Order, another vendor appears.

The Reason

  • The Contact record for the vendor has a non-unqiue Account number. To prevent mis-matches, each Account number (for Vendor records) must be unique.

How to Find the Wrong Vendor

Update your Vendor's Contact Record

  1. Locate the Vendor record in your Contacts.

  2. If you have not been assigned an account number or you don’t know the account number, then enter their phone number.

    The Account field must be unique to prevent mis-matches!

    In order for vendors to link to the Price Codes file, an entry must appear in the Account # field. This Account # will appear on the PO.
