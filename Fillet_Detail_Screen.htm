Fillet Detail Screen

Work Orders (form view) > Framing Materials section > Fillet Detail tab

Tip: The tabs at the top of each Work Order Detail Screen allow you to move through the Work Order in different ways.

Fillets Detail Screen Explained

  • The Fillet Detail screen gives you with the ability to dictate the location of any fillets used with mats.

  • Fillets used with frames should be notated within the Frame1 Detail screen.

Fillet Details Screen

  • The details for each fillet selected for a Work Order are located in the Fillet Details. 

  • These are similar details that can be found within the Price Code File. Review the Online Help pages regarding the Price Codes to get more details about pricing and moulding details.

  • The two other buttons on this menu are there to make modifications to the sizing or pricing.

Modify Button

  • Click to bring up another window that you can use to modify the pricing and sizing information for a moulding.

Adjust Margin of Top Mat Button

  • Click to bring up another window that will allow you to manually edit your current top mat margin.
