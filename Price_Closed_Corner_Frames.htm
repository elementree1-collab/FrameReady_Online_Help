Price Closed Corner Frames

Main Menu > Price Codes > Extra Group

  • You may want to order closed corner frames, also known as finished corner frames, from certain suppliers  such as Rhonda Feinman Custom Frames and GoldLeaf Framemakers of Santa Fe.

  • Strictly speaking, these are not classified as 'readymades', but priced as joined frames. Finished craftsman style, water gilded gold frames, steel frames or frames from historical periods all fall into this category.

  • A number of closed corner frames make extensive use of comp corners, gessoed mitre joints, hardwood mortice and tenon joints custom made to exact specifications.

  • The pricing of compos should be placed in the Extra group and selected as required.
