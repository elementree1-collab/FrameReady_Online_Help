How to Add Opening Sales for a FrameReady Contact

  • Is there a field where I can record how much a customer spent in my shop before I started using FrameReady?

  • Yes. It's called the Opening Sales field in the Contacts file > Invoices/Rewards tab (bottom half of screen).

  • If you are implementing FrameReady in an established shop, you may use this field to record how much the customer spent prior to you implementing FrameReady.

  • The amount you enter here is also entered into the Total Invoices box at the bottom.
