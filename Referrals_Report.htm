Referrals Report

Contact > Referral tab > Print Report button

Print a report showing which customers have referred new people to your shop.

How to Print a Referrals Report

  1. In the Contacts (form view), open the Referrals tab.

    It does     not matter which Contact is selected.

  2. Click the Print Report button.

  3. A print preview of the report appears.

  4. Click Continue.

  5. The report includes the following fields: Customer ID, Customer name, the customer they referred, the referral date and the amount spent.
