Switch to Automatic Inventory

If you have already entered inventory in your Price Codes and wish to clear those entries and start over, then follow these instructions.

How to Switch to Auto Update and Start Fresh

Step 1: Take Inventory of the Price Codes

  1. Go to your Price Codes.

  2. Click the Moulding sidebar button to find all of the wood, metal, liner, fillet etc. items in your database. Switch to List View.

    You can then do the same for each of the other sidebar button groups listed on the left side of the Form View screen.

    or

    You may want to search for only those items that are in stock: click Find and enter  >0  into the Qty field. Optionally select a Supplier if you want to know how Larson Juhl, for example, you have.

  3. Click the Inventory List View button to see a list of your stock.

  4. Optionally, use the Sort button to sort the items by location or quantity or whatever will help you in your manual inventory.

  5. Use the Print List button to print a document to use in conjunction with your physical inventory count.

    Caution: This may consume many, many sheets of printer paper.

  6. In the Inventory List screen, enter your counted amounts into the Actual field.



    Actual fields with a value of zero will replace the value in the Quantity field with zero. Also, when a number is entered into the Actual field, the difference is automatically calculated in the Variance field. This provides you with a variance report of what you thought you had in inventory and what is actually there (large variances should be investigated).

    Once you have entered your numbers into the Actual column, then you can move all of the numbers from the Actual field into the Qty field, see below:

Step 2: Move the Actual Values into the Quantity Column

To move the numbers from the Actual field to the Qty field:

  1. Go to Main Menu, in the Products section, and click the Clear Inventory Fields button.

  2. Click on the button: Move the values from the Actual field to Quantity field and clear the Actual field.

Do NOT click on the Clear All Retail Items button. This will delete the amount in ALL of the Quantity fields.

  1. When finished, a list appears showing all Products which have had the value in the Actual field moved to the Qty field.
