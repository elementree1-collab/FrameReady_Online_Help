Create a Tax Exempt Work Order

Work Orders

If a customer qualifies for any type of retail sales tax exemption, use these steps.

How to Create a Tax Exempt Work Order

  1. From the Work Order, in the Price section, open the Options tab (upper right).

  2. Enter the Exception Number.



    FrameReady adjusts the Work Order's tax calculation and saves the Tax Exemption number in the Contact's record.

Tip: Alternatively, you can enter the Tax Exemption number on the Contact's record OR directly on an Invoice.
