Always see your Contacts

Main Menu > Contacts section > Options tab

An option is available to always leave the Contacts window open or visible.

Caution: This feature is not available when Auto Zoom is turned on.

How to Keep Your Contacts Visible Onscreen

  1. On the Main Menu, in the Contacts section, open the Options tab.

  2. Check the Do not hide Contacts when moving to other files (not available when Auto Zoom is turned on) box.



    Now your contacts will always be visible in a separate floating window. This is useful with large screen monitors where the Contacts windows is moved to a corner of the screen and always available.
