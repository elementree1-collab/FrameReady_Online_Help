Opening FrameReady everyday – Quick Opening Tip

When you open FrameReady at the start of your day, it asks you to select the account and password for the level in which you wish to operate.

Tip: If an account other than level1 – level4 appears in the Account Name field, then delete the entry and then type in the account you want, e.g. level2 or 2-level2 or multi-site location. Enter the corresponding password.

Quick Opening Tip

Set your computer to always enter the account name to use when opening:

Important: Do not use these steps if you are using multiple receipt printers, i.e. the Account Name is "Register1" or similar.

  1. If FrameReady is open, then completely exit out of the program.

  2. Double-click the FileMaker Pro icon on Dock or Desktop.

  3. Windows: in the menu bar, click Edit and choose Preferences...

    Mac: in the menu bar, click FileMaker Pro and choose Preferences...

  4. In the General tab, click in the User Name field and enter the level that you wish to use (replacing that which is already there).

    Important! Make sure there are no spaces before or after.

  5. Click OK.

    FrameReady automatically enters that account name when it opens, saving you some typing.
