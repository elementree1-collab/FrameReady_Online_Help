FrameReady: Technical Support

We all know what it’s like working with IT products, some days everything is fine and the next, something strange happens and you don’t even know what the problem is!

Benefits of your active FrameReady Subscription

  1. You can phone or email the the FrameReady Team for assistance between 9:00 AM – 5:30 PM Eastern Monday to Friday.

  2. You receive matboard and moulding pricing updates to your FrameReady program.

  3. You receive notification of any major upgrades, keeping the software up-to-date and competitive.

  4. You receive minor software revisions throughout the year, if they are released.

Tip: If there are components to FrameReady that you don’t know how to use or if you are a new user, then this online help website can assist you. We want you to be able to utilize FrameReady to its fullest capacity, this way your business will benefit greatly and our product will be able to work better for you.

FrameReady Subscription (January 2020)

Active FileMaker License Required

  • Effective January 1st, 2020, an active FileMaker client license is required in order to use FrameReady.

  • Claris International Inc. (formerly FileMaker Inc.) now require you pay for a FileMaker license and that we are not permitted to provide support to customers who have an inactive license.

  • To address this, we are simplifying and unifying our pricing structure. There is now a single license fee which combines the old "subscription" and "maintenance" into one fee.

  • Existing users have the option to pay annually or monthly; new users are enrolled into the monthly.

FrameReady on Multiple Computers

  • Effective January 1st, 2020, changes are being driven by our software provider, FileMaker Inc. (Claris International Inc.).

  • FileMaker has made a change to their platform that we now must comply with:

    "Peer-To-Peer" non server-based sharing is no longer being supported.

    Instead, FileMaker Inc. requires the use of FileMaker Server software to use FrameReady on multiple computers.

  • There will be no additional initial cost for using the server version of FrameReady but, beginning in 2020, we will be encouraging all of our users to purchase a server computer in order to host their FrameReady solution.

  • We will continue to support peer-to-peer users until the end of 2020.

Coming in 2021

  • Effective January 1st, 2021, we can only provide support to FrameReady users who have FileMaker Server installed whereas peer-to-peer users will be unable to receive support.
