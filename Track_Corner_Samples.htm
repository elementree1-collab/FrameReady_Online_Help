Track Corner Samples

Main Menu > Contacts

When a customer or designer borrows a sample, you want to know: who has it, when they took it, and has it been returned. You could write it on a post-it note, but, there is a better way!

How to Track Corner Supplies out on Loan

  1. In the Contacts file, open the Keywords tab, and create a keyword called "Sample on Loan"

  2. When a customer borrows a sample(s), go to their record in the Contacts file. Click on the keyword "Sample on Loan" and, in the Date/Notes tab, enter the sample numbers in the Art Type/Notes field.

    FrameReady automatically enters the current date when you leave the field.

  3. When the sample is returned, uncheck the keyword and delete the line in the tab or type "Returned" beside it.

  4. If you want to know who has samples out on loan, search for the keyword "Sample on Loan"

  5. If you want to know who has a particular sample, search for the keyword AND the sample number under the Date/Notes tab.

Identifying Corner Samples

FrameReady allows for the tracking of which frame and matboard samples you have on display.

  • When new samples arrive mark the Matboard Sample or Moulding Sample checkbox (in the upper right corner of the Price Codes file).

  • At any time, you can Perform a Find for samples from any particular supplier.

  • This makes it easy when it comes time to find discontinued samples: print out a list, find them on your wall and remove them. This is a terrific time saver. No use selling a frame to someone if it’s not available.

Corner Sample Management

As you pull off the sample to dust it, call the number over to the person at the computer.

Have them do these two things in FrameReady:

  1. Find the moulding sample in the Price Codes file.

  2. If it is a valid sample, tick the Moulding Sample checkbox (top right corner).

This will save you valuable time when the list of discontinued (Not Verified) items come up later. You will be able to perform a Find for all records which are identified as Not Verified (in the Validation field) AND have an X in the Moulding Sample box. This gives you a short list of your discontinued samples which you can print. The samples can be quickly removed from your wall rather than waiting for a sales rep, or customer, to find it for you.

If ithe item is discontinued (or not verified), you can remove it before dusting and free up valuable wall space (which should not be wasted on products that can’t be sold). But don’t toss it in the rubbish. Consider donating it to your local high school shop class or Habitat for Humanity ReStore in your area. It’s amazing what crafts-people make out of them.

Optional: if you want to identify on which sample display wall or in which column the sample is hanging, you can type that into the Price Codes as well. If you are not using the Location field to identify where the inventory is stored, you can enter the sample location there instead, e.g. W12 (for west wall 12th column). Or you can use the field under the Image tab.

Tip: In the Image Tab you can store a photograph of the sample for reference.
