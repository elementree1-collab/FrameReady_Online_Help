FrameReady Payments Integration Documentation

FrameReady now has a new Credit Card processing solution called FrameReady Payments! This simplifies the credit card processing solutions at your Frameshop in many diﬀerent ways.

 

To connect to your FrameReady Payments account there are just a few simple things that you will need to do to get going.

 

 

Connecting to your FrameReady Payments Account

 

First, go to the Setup Data on the main menu at the top right and then click on the Fiscal tab. In the Credit Card processing section at the bottom of this tab select the FrameReady Payments button and then click on the ‘FrameReady Payments Integration Settings’ button to begin setting up your connection.

 

 

Once you are on the FrameReady Payments Integration settings page there is a few pieces of critical information that you will need to enter into the system before you can start connecting your account.

 

 

First of all, you need to enter your ‘Account ID’ into the top field. This can be located within your account portal at http://payments.frameready.com

 

To locate your Account ID, go to the Account menu at the top right of the screen and right below it when you click on it, you will see your account name and a dash and a number directly below it. In the example below, the account ID for this account is “201212”

 

 

 

After you have entered your Account ID into FrameReady settings page, you will need to enter your email address and password for your account that you use to login to your portal at http://payments.frameready.com

 

After you have your Account ID, email and password entered into the system, you are ready to connect to your FrameReady Payments account. Click on the black button that says “Connect to FrameReady Payments”

 

 

 

This will collect the information that you have entered and submit it to our API and provide a connection. After the account has successfully connected, you will see an authorization code populate in the Authorization code field near the bottom of the screen. The tab at the top will also turn green and will display the message “Connected to FrameReady Payments”

 

 

Connecting a Terminal

 

Now that you are connected to your FrameReady Payments account, you are ready to register your terminal so it is connected to your workstations installation of FrameReady.

 

Once you have received your P5 or S1F2 terminal device from FrameReady Payments, power the device on.

 

For the P5 Terminal, Open up the Payments application on the device. You will see a screen on the terminal once you open the application that says “Terminal Registration”

Back in FrameReady, below the heading that says Terminal Registration, there will be a blue box that has a 3 word code inside of it separated by dashes. This is the code that you will be prompted to enter in FrameReady once you follow the “Register A Terminal” process in the system. To begin the process, click on the “Register A Terminal” button in FrameReady and it will begin the process.

 

For the S1F2 Terminal, after you power on the terminal, select your store location from the list of store locations on the terminal and wait for the terminal to load to the Home Screen where you will see a settings icon at the bottom of the screen and a transactions icon.

 

Locate the serial number on the back of the S1F12 Terminal and this is what you will enter on the terminal registration screen in FrameReady for the S1F2. The serial number will be marked on the back of the terminal with the label “S/N”

 

The first dialog that you will see pop up is a dialog to select your location that you are wanting to pair the terminal with. You can add your locations for all of your oﬃces in the http://payments.frameready.com portal by clicking on the Account > Profile section in the top navigation.

 

Below Bank Accounts is a section for Locations. You can add a new location by clicking the + Add Location button. Add all of your locations here before you connect your terminal so that you can link the terminal with the correct oﬃce location.

 

 

Once you click on the Register a Terminal button it will pop up the dialog with all of your oﬃce locations. Select the oﬃce location that you want to pair the terminal with by clicking on the arrow on the left hand side of the location record.

 

 

Once you select the location, a dialog box will pop up asking you to enter the terminal registration number that is on your terminal app screen. Enter the number into this box and click the OK button to continue.

 

 

For the S1F2 Terminal you will need to enter the serial number of the terminal at this dialog instead of the terminal registration code.

The integration will check the number you entered and make sure it matches the code on the terminal screen. Once it it done pairing, you will see the screen on the terminal change from the “Terminal Registration” screen to the main processing screen with all of the logos for Apple Pay, Samsung Pay etc..

Now that you have connected your account and registered your terminal with FrameReady you are ready to begin processing payments on invoices.

 

 

Processing Payments

 

To process a payment on an invoice you need to navigate to the Invoices section of the program and locate an invoice that has balance left on it still. Once you have navigated to an invoice that you would like to process a payment on, click the button that says “Data Entry” on the Invoice to enter into data entry mode.

 

Once you are in Data Entry mode, click on the button that says “Enter a Payment” to bring up the new payment dialog window. Once the payment dialog window is up on the screen you will need to enter an amount for the payment you want to process as well as select the Tender (type of card) that you are going to be using to process the payment. Once you have entered this information, you will need to click on the button to the right with the green text that says “Process CC Transaction”.

 

 

 

Once you click this button it will activate the FrameReady Payments integration to begin the payment process. A new dialog window will pop up with the amount of the payment and other details about the payment.

 

You will be prompted with a dialog in the FrameReady program asking you if this transaction is a Chip/Swipe (card present) transaction or a Manual (card not present) transaction. Select the option that you would like to use to process the transaction to continue.

 

(If you have selected Interac payments for Canada, you will not be prompted for Chip/Swipe or Manual, Interac payments require you to insert the card into the S1F2 pin pad)

 

If you would like to cancel the payment after you have selected the payment type and the terminal lights up with the “Insert Your Card” screen, you can click the “Cancel” button on the pop up screen in FrameReady in the top right corner. A dialog window will come up and ask you if you are sure you would like to

 

cancel the payment. If you continue with this, the payment will be canceled and the terminal will return to the original dormant screen.

 

If you select the “Chip/Swipe” option, the terminal will light up with the amount of the payment that you entered to process asking you to insert or tap the card to process the payment. Insert or tap your card and the terminal will process the payment. If the payment is successful the terminal will say payment success and you will get a pop up in FrameReady letting you know the payment was a success.

You will need to use your finger to tap into these fields and enter the required information about the card you are using.

After the payment has been successfully processed, a new record will be added for the payment on the invoice in the payments portal at the bottom of the screen. The payment record in the payments portal will show the Amount, the tender (card type), as well as the Reference number which will be the FrameReady Payments “transaction id”. This is the ID that can be used to refer to the payment in your payments portal http://payments.frameready.com

 

In the payments portal, click on the “Payments” link at the top left of the screen and it will load a list of all of your payments. To the left hand side in the list of the records you will see a field for “Transaction ID”. Here is where you can locate a payment based on the stored reference number on the payment in FrameReady.

 

You will also see a field on the payment records to the left that says “Status”. This will tell you the status of the payment as to whether it has been successfully processed and captured, declined, refunded etc… You can also click on any of the payments in the list and it will bring up a dialog with more information on that payment as well as a detailed payment log.

 

You can also click on the “Edit” button on the payment in the payments portal in FrameReady to open the detail view of the payment you just processed. You will be able to see here some more information about the payment such as the Name on the Card, the Last of The Card and the charge type.

 

 

Processing a Refund

 

To process a refund on a payment made with the FrameReady Payments integration, you need to click on the “Edit” button on the payment record in the FrameReady payments portal.

 

When you click on the edit button it will open up the detail view of the payment record. Once you are here, click on the “Tender” dropdown and change the tender from the card type that you used to process the payment to “Refund - (Card Type)”

 

 

Once you have changed the Tender to the Refund value matching the card type, click the “Process CC Transaction” button just as you would if you were processing a payment. Once you click this, the program will realize that you are attempting to process a refund and will ask you if you are sure you want to process a refund.

 

 

After you continue, it will ask you what the reason for the refund is in a pop up a dialog. Enter the reason for the refund and click to continue. The next dialog box that will pop up will ask you what amount you would like to refund on the payment. The dialog will default to the original payment amount but you can change the amount to refund by entering any amount less than the payment amount and greater than zero.

 

 

If you enter a refund amount that is less than the original payment amount, the program will prompt you and let you know that you are processing a partial refund and ask you if you are sure and do you want to continue.

 

 

If you continue with the prompt, the integration will process the refund and you will be notified that the refund or partial refund was successful.

 

(If you are using Interac as your payment tender for Canada, it will prompt you to insert your card. Interac refunds require that the card be inserted into the S1F2 pin pad in order to process the refund)

In the payments portal in FrameReady, you will see a new record that has been added for the refund. This record will have the same transaction ID as the payment but after the transaction ID it will say “(Refunded)” or “(Partially Refunded)” so you can tell what the payment is in the portal. You will also see that the payment amount for the payment in the payment portal is a negative amount. This will balance out the invoice for the amount that has been refunded.

 

 

 

In the payments portal at http://payments.frameready.com you will see the status change for the original payment record from “Captured” to either “Refunded” or “Partially Refunded”. It will not create a new record like FrameReady does but will just update the status on the original payment record.

 

You can click on the “Edit” button on the payment in the FrameReady payments portal to see the detail view of the refund payment. It will show you at the bottom of the screen the “Refund Reason” that was entered for the particular refund in case these refund reasons need to be referred to at any point to reported.

 

 

Processing a Refund on its own Invoice

 

One of the common methods of processing refunds in FrameReady is to create a new invoice and then process the refund for a previous invoices payment on that new invoice.

 

We have now added support in FrameReady payments to be able to process refunds on their own individual invoices. After you have created a new invoice for your refund, enter the line item detail for the refund line item and then enter the quantity as a negative number (ex. -1)

 

Enter the price on the line item as a positive amount and then save your invoice and add the customer. Your invoice should come out with a negative balance once you are finished entering the line item because of the negative quantity.

 

 

Before you add your refund payment to the invoice, you will need to navigate to the original invoice and copy the reference number for the original payment.

Copy the payment reference number because you will need to paste it into the reference number on the new payment.

 

Once your invoice is ready, hit the Data Entry button and then click the Enter a Payment to add a new Payment.

 

Make sure to select the tender of “Refund - (whatever card type)” in the tender dropdown box. The amount of the payment will default to the balance which

 

should be left as a negative number. Then, in the Reference field, you will need to paste the payment reference number from the original payment that you copied. This allows the Payments Integration to reference the original payment and apply that refund to the payment.

 

Once you have entered all of the necessary details and pasted the reference number from the original payment, you can hit the “Process CC Transaction” button on the right hand side to process the refund. Once the refund has been processed the balance of the invoice should balance back out to zero.
