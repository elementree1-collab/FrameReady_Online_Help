Print a Blank Invoice

Main Menu > Print Blank Invoice Form button

If you need to do sales on the road or at someplace where you don’t have access to a computer/printer, then FrameReady allows you to print out some blank Invoice forms ahead of time.

How to Print a Blank Invoice

  1. On the Main Menu in the Invoices section, click the Print Blank Invoice Form button.

  2. A dialog box appears.

  3. Click One-Part or Two-Part.

  4. A print preview appears.

  5. Click Continue.
