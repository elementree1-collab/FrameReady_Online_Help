New FileMaker Features

Pop-over

  • An outlined, pop-up box appears above of a small area of your current screen when you click in a field. There is no option for a close or done button: to close the pop-over, simply click in another area of the screen. Pop-overs have been added to fields which you previously could not click into.

Hidden Objects

  • The ability to hide objects on the screen when they are not in use. For example, the Site number field will not display when FrameReady is set to single user or the CMC button will not display if it is set to none.

Added:

  1. Support for 1920x1080 pixel monitors; Auto Zoom can use 150% zoom in 1080 pixel displays. Mac users may set the Dock to Auto-Hide or to the right.

  2. In Find screens, on Menu bar at top of screen: Edit, Insert, Requests.

  3. In Find screens, status toolbar to automatically appear at top of screen.

  4. In Currency drop down list, added Rand as an option to select.

Modified:

Contacts
