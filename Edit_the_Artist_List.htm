Edit the Artist List

Main Menu > Products > Artist tab > Edit Artists button

How do I edit the list of artists?

How to Edit the Artist List

  1. Go into any Contact record and open the Artwork tab.

  2. Click the Edit Artists button.

  3. A list of artists appears with a Delete button and Edit Spelling button beside each name.

  4. The list is sorted by Artist and also displays any customers who have a preference for that artist. But, if the Artist was entered into the Products file using the New Artist button, then their name appears without a customer.

How to Delete an Artist

  • When you click Delete, a warning box appears to confirm your intent to delete the artist. When an artist is deleted, the entire list of their name will be removed from the screen you are viewing.

  • When an artist is deleted, their name is removed from the pop-up Artist menu on the Products, Work Order and Contacts files.

  • The name will also be removed from the Artist field of all customers who had previously purchased their work.

  • The artist’s name will remain on any artwork still in the Products file and on invoices of their previously sold art.

How to Edit Spelling

  • The Edit Spelling button opens a dialog box where you can type in the correct name of the artist. A second dialog box  asks you to confirm the change. When completed, a report shows of how many times the artist's name was found and changed, as well as how many records were searched. The spelling will be changed on the customer file.

  • When the spelling is changed, the new spelling appears in the pop-up Artist menu in the Products and Contacts file and also in the listing of preferred artists for each customer who had previously purchased their artwork in Artist tab.

  • The spelling of the name will not be changed on any previously created Work Orders or Invoices.

How to Add an Artist

  • Clicking the New Artist button presents this dialog box:

  • Enter the name of a new artist. The name you type into the field will automatically be entered into the record you were in when you clicked the New Artist button. The new artist’s name will be added to the pop-up menu in the Artist field.
