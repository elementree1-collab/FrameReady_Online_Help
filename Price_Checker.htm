Price Checker

Main Menu > Price Codes section (lower right)

The Price Checker button is a quick and easy way to give a customer a quote on the retail price per foot of any moulding without creating a Work Order.

  • The Price Checker button is a quick and easy way to give a customer a quote on the retail price per foot of any moulding without creating a work order.

  • You can also use it to compare the retail prices of different moulding samples for a customer who is looking for a less expensive option in their design. Or, for any time when you are not on a Work Order and need to know the retail price per foot of a moulding.

How to Use the Price Checker

  1. Click Price Checker icon (lower right hand corner)

  2. This dialog box appears.

  3. Scan a barcode from the back of the corner sample or type the moulding number or the first part of it into the Item field.

  4. Click Search.

    If more than one moulding matches the search criteria then a list appears from which to choose.

  5. Click the Item number you want, or click the Find button to return to the Price Checker input windodw.

    If you wish to perform another find, simply click in the     Item field and repeat the process.

    The Retail Price per foot appears based on the price structure you have set up in the Price Codes file (which is why the background color of this dialog box is the same as the Price Codes file.)

  6. Click Done to close.
