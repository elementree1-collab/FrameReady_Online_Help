View Inventory from a Specific Date

Main Menu > Price Codes section > Find Price Code Item button

There is no way to establish an inventory count at a specific date (FrameReady can only capture the inventory at the present moment); however these steps can provide some raw data.

  • FrameReady can only capture and display the inventory at the present moment. 

  • However, these steps can provide some raw data. It is a manual process and may not scale well for hundreds of products.

How to find and view raw Inventory data

  1. On the Main Menu, in the Invoice section, click the Sales Reports button and choose Other...

  2. The Custom Report window appears.

  3. In the date field, enter the day after the target date followed by three dots  …

    This searches for all products sold after your target date.

  4. Click the Proceed button.

  5. The first report is "Tax1 sales by category", click Continue then Cancel; we don't need this report.

  6. The next report is "Retail Items Sold." We need this one, so print it out.

    Now we have the Qty of items sold after the target date.

  7. Next we want to know the current Qty status of items in the Products file.

    From the Main Menu, in the Products section, click the Inventory Reports button.

  8. In the "Detailed Report sorted by" section, click Category and print the report.

    This report shows the current Qty. 

  9. Match the item from the 1st report, e.g. 3 were sold.

    The 2nd report, for example, says there are 7 in inventory.

    This means the Qty at the target date was 10.

  10. When finished, the Main Menu appears.

Important

  • You may also need to take into consideration any inventory that was ordered during that time and that was added to the inventory. That amount would need to be deducted.
