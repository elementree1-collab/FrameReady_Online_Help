Add Plaque Mounting

Main Menu > Price Codes

If your shop offers plaque mounting, then you can add the service into FrameReady, set up your pricing, and go.

How to Add Plaque Mounting

Tip: If you price by the square inch or square foot, then we recommend you use the Mounting group.

  1. Click the New Price Code Item button and choose Mounting.

  2. Enter an Item name, e.g.  Plaque Mounting  or  Plaque Float 

Tip: You may want to use an abbreviation, for example "PLQ" in front of all plaque services. For example, "PLQ - Matted" to easily identify, find and price plaque mounting. Also, when you enter it on a Work Order, use the magnifying glass icon and type "PLQ" to see only the plaque mount services.

  1. Add your wholesale Cost per unit, for example 0.31 cents per 1 square inch.

    If, instead, you are adding a retail cost, then make sure you add a Markup of  1 .

  2. In the Dimensions field, choose the size on which the wholesale cost is based. For example,   1 x 1  to price by the square inch,  12 x 12  to price by the foot, etc.

Tip: Use Edit… to add a size that is not in the list. The list is not sorted automatically so you will need to place the size into the appropriate area on the list, for example, 1 x 1 at top of list.

  1. Open the Mounting Pricing tab and adjust the Markup to your needs.

  2. Optionally add a Set Price to cover your setup or any post-processing you might need to do per image.

  3. Examine the Price Sampler to the right to confirm the accuracy of the pricing. Adjust as needed.

    If the Price Sampler is empty, then click the Reset button to refresh it.

  4. Open the Other tab.

    Pricing information added here appears on the Work Order's Other section.

  5. Click in the Unit of Measure field and select the unit matching your chosen Dimensions (above), e.g.  Sq_In 

  6. Add the price of your chosen unit of measure into the Price/UOM field. You may need to manually calculate this value using your Markup and Cost.

  7. Optionally add a Set Price to cover your setup or any post-processing you might need to do per image.

  8. In the Min Price field, if applicable, add a minimum dollar amount per print job.

  9. If your Retail Price comes from a Code Table, then choose the table in the Code field.

    Important: this overrides all other price settings.

  10. Add a Note if applicable.

Important: As with all Price Code items, it is your responsibility to enter the costs and update them manually when necessary.

Test your Pricing on a Work Order

  1. Create a new Work Order and enter test dimensions.

    Do not add mat margins at this time.

  2. In Mount/Stretch, choose plaque mounting.

  3. Click the underlined Other button.

    The Other Items window appears.

  4. In the Other Items window, click the Add Item button.

    The Add 'Other' Line Item window appears.

  5. Enter your plaque mounting using the same test dimensions and click the Other Pricing line item from the lower-right table.

  6. The Mount/Stretch and Other cost should be identical.

  7. Next, adjust the Mat Margins and observe how the two costs differ.
