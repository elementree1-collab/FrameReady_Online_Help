Link your FrameReady Contacts to your Constant Contact Lists.

Market to your FrameReady customers using your Constant Contact account

FrameReady offers the option to import your Constant Contact Lists and then assign or remove Contacts to your email marketing campaigns.

  • You can import your Lists from Constant Contact into FrameReady.

  • You can assign Contacts from your FrameReady Contacts to one or multiple different Constant Contact Lists.

  • You can also unsubscribe your Contacts from these Lists at any time in FrameReady.

  • These assignments are directly recorded in your Constant Contact account for ease of use. This makes managing your mailing lists a breeze since you can do it directly in FrameReady without having to do it in multiple places.
