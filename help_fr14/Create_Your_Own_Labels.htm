Create Your Own Labels

Due to variations in printers and print drivers, labels may not line up as they should on a label sheet. In such cases, the label layout will need to be adjusted to accommodate your particular printer. This procedure can only be accomplished using a full version of FileMaker Pro software.

How to Create your own Labels

  1. Open the Product Labels file.

  2. In the menu bar, click Perform and choose Restore Full Menus.

  3. Also in the menu bar, click View and choose Status Toolbar.

  4. In the menu bar, click View and choose Layout Mode.

  5. This is the screen you should see.

Tip: If you cannot see the layout number in the top left corner, then you will need to add it to the Status Toolbar by clicking View > Customize Status Toolbar… and then drag the icon of the double arrows upward and onto the grey display area at the top of the screen and place it in the top left corner.

  1. In the top left corner, look for the Layout field. Click 'Data Entry' to see a drop down list of available label layouts. Select one of these to modify or to duplicate. The checkmark indicates the current layout.

  2. Here is what Layout 5160 might look like:

  3. Note: the fields with  <<  (less than) and  >>  (greater than) symbols are called "Merge Fields". They expand (horizontally) to use the room available. Regular fields have bounding boxes around them and can only fill the area you give them. Merge Fields are more popular in labels.

    The black rectangle is the barcode.

  4. Use the T icon (Text tool) on the toolbar to have your mouse appear as a text-cursor. 

  5. Click into a field to make items bold or larger in size. Once your text is highlighted, you can then use the Inspector to change the size, style, etc. 

How to Add a Field

  1. Click on a blank area in the label.

  2. In the menu bar, click Insert and choose Merge Field…

  3. The Specify Field selection box appears. 

  4. In the dropdown menu (above the list), is the file from which different fields can be selected.

    The Current Table is "Labels," but you can select any other Related Table, for example WO (which stands for Work Order). The WO list shows all files available on the Work Order screen.

  5. When you have finished customizing your label, take note of the Layouts number (top left corner). In this example, it is layout 3 of 15.

  6. To exit: in the Layout field, change to layout Data Entry (top of the list).

  7. Click Save to keep your changes or click Don’t Save if you do not want the label to change.



    Avoid checking the Save layout automatically (do not ask) field.

  8. In the menu bar, click View and choose Browse Mode, then click View and choose Status Toolbar to turn it off.

  9. Add your label to the list on the right side of the Print Labels screen if it is not already listed. Be sure to identify the layout number.
