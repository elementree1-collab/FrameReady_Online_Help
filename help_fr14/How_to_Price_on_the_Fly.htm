How to Price On-the-Fly, or Price Override

If you have a new moulding corner sample and wish to use it in a Work Order, but have not had time to download the latest pricing update in FrameReady, then here's how to sell the moulding on the fly.

  1. On the Work Order, in the Framing Components section: click the Blue Button to open a dialog where you can enter a Moulding Number and Retail Price for the entire frame.

  2. Enter the Moulding Number (no vendor suffix is required.)

  3. Enter the Retail Price you wish to charge.

  4. Click OK.
