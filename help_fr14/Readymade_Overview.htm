Readymade Overview

Main Menu > Products > Form View > Readymade tab

FrameReady differentiates between supplier-provided readymades and those you prepare in-house using your own materials.

Tip: To add your own readymade frames, see: Creating Your Own Readymade Frame Product

Readymade Overview

  • Oval, circle and readymade rectangular frames (e.g. Inline Ovals) are stored in the Products file rather than the Price Codes file (because they are supplier-provided and not prepared in-house).

  • Readymades are treated as a retail item with a wholesale cost and a fixed retail price.

  • In-stock Readymades may be used in custom framing jobs by entering them in the Readymade field (below the Frame1 field) on the Work Order.

  • An inventory can be kept of Readymade frames using the Qty on Hand field. The inventory is automatically updated when an Invoice is printed out or when a Work Order is posted to an Invoice.

  • Add your own readymades into FrameReady by first pricing it out in a Work Order, then converting it into a Product item.
