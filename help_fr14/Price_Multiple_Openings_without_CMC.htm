Main Menu > Work Orders

How to Price a Multiple Opening mat on a Work Order without using a CMC

  1. On the Main Menu, in the Work Order section, open the Options tab.

  2. Click the More Options… button.

  3. In the Computerized Mat Cutter list, select the Wizard MatDesigner radio button.

  4. Click Done.

  5. On the Work Order, click the CMC button.

    This will launch the MatDesigner software which will allow you to design a multiple opening mat. Windows only.

  6. Click the Back to POS button.

or

  1. Measure the outside dimension of the items to be framed (or window opening) including any spaces between.

    For example, Four 8"x10" openings, arranged in a square, with 2 inches of mat between, would have a width of 18 inches and a height of 22 inches. This gives you the overall window size.

  2. In the Width and Height field, type in these dimensions.

  3. Add applicable mat margins.

  4. Enter the number of openings to be cut in Extra Openings.

    For example, if there are 6 openings, then enter 5 in the box (because one opening is always assumed).

  5. Enter your mat color number.

  6. Draw out your design on a blank piece of paper.

  7. You may wish to Lock the price after you have quoted a price to the customer.

  8. Now print the Work Order onto the back of your sketch. Or take a digital image of the layout and add it to the work order under the picture tab (lower right corner).
