Pricing Options and Defaults Overview

Price Codes File > Moulding Group (Sidebar Button) > Frame Pricing Tab > Pricing Options and Defaults Button

The Pricing Options and Defaults screen is a specialized, separate file used as a reference for vendor price updates and for storing your own unique formulas.

FrameReady Webinar: Pricing Options and Defaults

Pricing Options and Defaults Sidebar Button

  • When FrameReady imports a new vendor record, such as a new moulding, it needs to know what pricing formula to apply to the new record.

  • So, FrameReady searches the Pricing Options and Defaults file for a Default Record - and when FrameReady finds one, the default record describes and instructs FrameReady on how to price the item. Therefore, it is important to understand and set up these default records correctly.

The following Groups support their own Pricing Options and Defaults:

  • Pricing Options and Defaults for Moulding

  • Pricing Options and Defaults for Matboard

  • Pricing Options and Defaults for Fabric

  • Pricing Options and Defaults for Glass

  • Pricing Options For Extra

  • Pricing Options and Defaults for Mounting

  • Pricing Options For Mat Design, Hardware, Extra, and Fitting

  • Pricing Options for Supplies
