Price Float Mounting

FrameReady has three options to price artwork to be 'floated' above the mat. You can enter it into the group of Mat Design or Mounting. Once entered into either of these groups, you have a third option of pricing it in the Other field on the Work Order.

How to Price Float Mounting

  1. Go to Price Codes and click the New Price Code Item button.

  2. Click Mat Design or Mounting; whichever works best for you.

    For example, if you are going to enter the backing matboard (which does not have an opening cut into it) in the Mountingfield, then you may want to enter the "Float Art" into the Mat Design group.

  3. Enter your description in the Item field. 

    For example, Float, Float-Hinged, etc.     Remember, entries appear in alphabetical order.

  4. In the Supplier field, choose In House .

  5. Enter your preferred pricing under the blue Mat Design or Mounting Pricing tab. 

Note: Remember that your pricing is based on the overall dimensions of the piece -- not just the area of the art. If you wish to price by the art area, then you need to set up the pricing in the Other tab and, on the Work Order, click on Other, enter this item, and enter the size of the art. Then, select your pricing structure in the box in the lower right corner.

  1. Check the Price Sampler (to the right) to ensure your pricing is set the way you wish.

    This item is now available when you go to the Work Order file and click in the Mat Design or Mount/Stretch field.
