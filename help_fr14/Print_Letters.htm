Print Letters

Print form letters to customers from within FrameReady's Contact file.

How to Print a One Page Letter

  • There are a two ways to produce a letter to send to one customer.

  • Let’s suppose you have a customer who needs a reminder letter to pick up her custom framing.

Option 1: How to Print a One Page Letter

Main Menu > Contacts section > Print Letter button

  1. On the Main Menu, in the Contacts section, click the Print Letter button.

  2. A dialog box appears:

  3. Click Single.

  4. A second dialog box appears:

  5. Select either name or company by using the drop-down list.

  6. Click Done.

  7. The screen changes to a letterhead format with the customer’s name and address inserted in the appropriate place.

Note: FrameReady automatically inserts the customer’s first name (or the title and last name) after the salutation “Dear”. If the letter is addressed to a company, to the attention of someone, then the name of the person in the Attention field is inserted in the salutation. If the Attention and the name fields are blank, then the salutation will read as “Dear Sir/Madam”.

  1. From the blue drop-down list, click on the letter template, e.g. "Thank you - Referral".

    The text of the template is inserted in the body of the letter.

  2. Make any changes to the text of your letter. Use the Spelling button to perform a spell check on your letter before printing.

Tip: To tab within the body of your letter, hold the down Option (Mac) or Ctrl (Windows) and then press the Tab key.

  1. Click the Print button.

  2. A print preview of the document appears.

  3. Click the Continue button.

    The print dialog box appears.

  4. Click Print (Mac) or OK (Windows).

    The screen returns to the Main Menu.

Option 2: How to Print a One Page Letter

Main Menu > Contacts > Find button

  1. On the Main Menu, in the Contacts section, click the Find Contact button.

  2. In the pink or blue arrow drop-down presents selection lists from which to choose the customer to whom you wish to send your letter. FrameReady inserts the ID# only.

  3. Click the Perform Find button.

  4. Click the Print Letter sidebar button.

    A letterhead format appears with the customer’s name and address inserted in the appropriate place.

  5. From the blue drop-down list, click on the letter template, e.g. "Thank you - Referral"

    The text of the template is inserted in the body of the letter.

  6. Make any changes to the text of your letter. Use the Spelling button to perform a spell check on your letter before printing.

Tip: To tab within the body of your letter, hold the down Option (Mac) or Ctrl (Windows) and then press the Tab key.

  1. When ready, click the Print button.

    A print preview appears.

  2. Click the Continue button.

    The print dialog box appears.

  3. Click Print (Mac) or OK (Windows).

    The screen returns to the Contacts screen.

How to Print a Bulk Mailing

  • A bulk mailing is done in exactly the same way as a single mailing except that you must choose search criterion for the mailing instead of an individual customer.

  • You can perform a bulk mailing from either the Main Menu or the Contacts file.

  • If you choose to print from the Main Menu, then you will need to have your records Marked or choose a Keyword for bulk mailing. If you choose to print from the Contacts file, then you will need to do a Find first before clicking on the Print Bulk Letters side bar button.

Tip: If your mailing requires a custom find (e.g., All customers who live in a certain zip code), then it is easier to do the mailing from the Contacts file.
