Log in to Time Cards

Main Menu > Time Cards

When you go to the Time Cards file, you are always taken to the Login Screen. You must select an account from the list (only active accounts are displayed).

After logging in, you are then taken to the Post Login Screen.

How to Login

  1. On the Main Menu, click the Time Cards icon.

  2. Click the Employee ID Number button for the account you are logging into.

  3. The password dialog appears.

  4. Enter your password and click OK.

    If you have forgotten the password, then click Cancel and click the Password Hint button.

  5. Next: Clock In / Clock Out
