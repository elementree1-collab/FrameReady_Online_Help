Add Outstanding Invoices

Main Menu > Invoices

You may have outstanding Invoices when you first implement FrameReady which you would like to enter into the system in order for them to show in your accounts receivable. If you wish, you may keep your existing Invoice numbers (recommended).

How to Enter Outstanding Invoices

  1. Create a new Invoice.

  2. Before entering any information into the Line Item Entry Screen, click Done.

  3. Change the Invoice number to match your outstanding invoice.

    Do not re-use an existing FrameReady invoice number!

  4. Change the Invoice Date to match your outstanding invoice.

  5. Click the purple Data Entry button.

    Enter the item(s) in the Description field (not the Item field) and the price into the Unit Price field. Change the Qty if it is greater than one.

  6. Select a Category from the drop-down list or type in the Category for this item.

    This is important for your sales reports.

  7. After entering all of the line items sold, enter any partial payments received.

    Click Enter a Payment and be sure to change the date to the date when the partial payment was actually made. Click Done.

  8. Click Done on the Line Item Screen to return to the Invoice screen.

Once you have entered all your existing Invoices, and you wish to continue with the same numbering sequence you have entered, then you will need to reset the Invoice numbers: Changing Invoice Numbering
