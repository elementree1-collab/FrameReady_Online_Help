How to Create a Custom Email Message

  1. On the Main Menu, in the Work Orders section, open the Options tab.

  2. Click the Edit Email Message button.

  3. Consult this article Setting up your Completion Email Message

  4. When you are finished making your changes, click the Done to exit.
