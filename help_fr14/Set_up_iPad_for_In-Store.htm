In-Store: Accessing FrameReady in your business on the iPad with Wi-Fi

Install FileMaker Go on your iPad before performing the following steps.

Opening FrameReady on the iPad

  1. Unlock your iPad.

  2. Make sure you have a wifi connection to the same network that your FrameReady host computer is on.

  3. Tap the FileMaker Go icon.

  4. At the bottom of the screen, tap the Hosts icon.

  5. If FileMaker Go finds your host computer, then you should see the FrameReady and MainMenu icons. Tap either and login as usual, e.g. level2 or level3.

  6. If FileMaker Go does not find your host computer, then tap the Hosts button (top left). If your host computer is listed, tap it. Otherwise tap the + icon and enter the IP address of your host computer.
