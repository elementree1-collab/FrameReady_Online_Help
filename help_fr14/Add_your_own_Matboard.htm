Price Codes

Main Menu > New Price Code Item button

Use this method if the matboard is from a company which does not participate in the Vendor Pricing Update Subscription service or if you are adding your own moulding.

How to Add your own Moulding

  1. On the Main Menu, in the Price Codes section, click the New Price Codes Item button.

  2. The New Price Code Item window appears.

  3. Select Matboard.

  4. Enter the matboard Item number.

    Be sure to enter a prefix which identifies the Supplier for the item, e.g. B105. Using a prefix ensures that the item will be updated when you download the vendor pricing update. The item number must be an exact match or the price will not be updated, e.g. B105 is not the same as B1-05.

    This is why you cannot have any duplicates: if you have two items with the same item number, then FrameReady will only enter the pricing of the first item on the Work Order.

  5. Dimensions: You MUST select the matboard dimensions.

  6. Wholesale Cost: Enter your wholesale cost (in the blue Cost field).

  7. Supplier: is the company who manufactures the product and may not necessarily be the vendor from whom you order it.

  8. Group: auto-entered when new record was created.

  9. Open the Matboard Pricing tab. Click Look Up My Default.

    The FrameReady default pricing is added to the item. You can adjust the pricing from there.

Any costs, markups, and retail prices used in the above instructions are for purposes of example only and not suggested pricing.
