Web Portal Contact Info

  • If a customer is logged in and clicks the Contact Information button on the Dashboard Home Screen, the Detail View for the customer appears.

  • On this screen they can view their contact info such as their name, address, emails, primary email, phones and shipping addresses.

Contact Information Section

  • On the left is the ‘Contact Information’ section.

  • Customers can edit this information by clicking directly into the fields with the information in them. They can then edit the text in the fields and when they are finished editing, click the Submit Changes button to save their changes in the Contact record.

  • After the changes are saved, it will refresh the Contact Info detail screen and the customer will see the updated information in the fields.

Emails Section

  • On the top right of the screen is the ‘Emails’ section.

  • Customers can click the + Add Email button to add a new email address or they can click the - button to delete an email address from their list of emails.

  • Additionally, customers can set one of their email addresses as primary by using the Primary checkbox. This will also update the primary email address for the Contact record in FrameReady.

Phone Numbers Section

  • Under the Emails section is the ‘Phone Numbers’ section.

  • Customers can add another phone number to their list of numbers in FrameReady or they can delete one of their phone numbers.

Shipping Addresses Section

  • Below the Phone Numbers section is the ‘Shipping Addresses’ section.

  • Customers can add a new shipping address by clicking the + Add Shipping Address button or can edit one of their shipping addresses by clicking the Edit button to the left of the address.

  • Customers can also delete any of their shipping addresses using the - button.

  • Once a customer adds a new shipping address, it is available to you in FrameReady (in the Invoices section for you to attach to any invoice that you are viewing by selecting from your available list of shipping addresses).
