Most of these fields are described in the Moulding Group Form View Screen therefore only the ones unique to these groups will be covered here.

Explanation of the Extra Group

The title box, EXTRA, identifies the Group of the item you are viewing.

Item Field

  • The name of the Item, e.g. Brass Corners, 2 ”.

  • Items in this group appear alphabetically in the drop-down list on the Work Order screen.

  • ake note of how you want the item to appear in the list. If ‘Brass’ written first, then all the Brass items will be grouped together. However, if ‘Corners’ is written first, then all of the Corners will be listed together. (E.g. Corners – brass 2 inch, Corners – gold 2 inch, etc.)

Manufacturer # Field

  • The manufacturer’s number which is used when the item is reordered. The Manufacturer # will appear on the Purchase Order.

  • Remember: It is Item # that appears in the drop-down list on the Work Order screen -- not the Manufacturer #.

Thickness Field

  • Used by the Work Order to calculate the overall thickness of the job and whether it exceeds the rabbet depth.

Cost Field

  • The wholesale cost you paid for this item. It is added to the total wholesale cost of a Work Order, however, it is not used in any calculations to determine the retail price of this item.

Set Price Field

  • A fixed dollar amount, e.g. Brass Corners $10.00, that is charged regardless of the size of the piece.

Supplier Drop-down

  • The name of the supplier or manufacturer.

Tip: If there is no supplier or if you are unsure, then select In House from the drop-down list.
