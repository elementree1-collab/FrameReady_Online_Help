Install FileMaker Go

In order to access FrameReady on an iPad, you will need to download the free FileMaker Go 19 app from the App Store.

How to Obtain FileMaker Go

  1. On your iPad, go to the App Store.

  2. Search for  FileMaker Go  and choose, if available, at least FileMaker Go 19.

  3. Tap the button labelled FREE and it will change to INSTALL APP.

  4. Tap INSTALL APP.

    Once the app starts downloading, you will be taken back to the iPad desktop where you will see an icon of the FileMaker Go app with a progress bar below it. Wait for the app to finish installing.

Now you are ready to use FrameReady on your iPad either In Store or Off Site.

  • Set up iPad for Off-Site

How to Update the FileMaker Go App

FrameReady 14 requires FileMaker Pro 19 and FileMaker Go 19. If you are using an older version of FileMaker Go, then follow these instructions to upgrade.

  1. Remove the old FileMaker Go from your iPad:

    • Locate the FileMaker Go and tap-hold down the icon.

    • After a few seconds the icon will dance and an X appears next to each of the icons.

    • Tap the X next to the FileMaker Go icon. Choose Delete.

  2. On your iPad, go to the App Store.

  3. Search for  FileMaker Go  and choose, if available, at least FileMaker Go 19.

  4. Tap the button labelled FREE and it will change to INSTALL APP.

  5. Tap INSTALL APP.

    Once the app starts downloading, you will be taken back to the iPad desktop where you will see an icon of the FileMaker Go app with a progress bar below it. Wait for the app to finish installing.
