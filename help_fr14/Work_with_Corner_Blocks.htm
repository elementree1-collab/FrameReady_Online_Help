Work with Corners in FrameReady

FrameReady can price any corner ornaments or composite corner in the Price Codes file under Extra group.

How to Add Corners to your Price Codes

  1. On the Main Menu, in the Price Codes section, click the New Price Code Item button.

  2. The New Price Code Item window appears.

  3. Choose Extra.

  4. Enter the Item name (avoid use of non-alphanumeric symbols, especially the single and double quote)

  5. Enter the wholesale Cost for one, e.g. $12.40, or all four corners, e.g. $49.60.

  6. Under Extra Pricing tab, click in the Set Price field.

    Choose one of the following methods of pricing:

    • Enter your retail price for one corner, e.g.  40.00 

    • Enter the retail price for installing all four corners with a dollar amount or a formula Cost x Markup, e.g.  cost * 3.5 

    • You could choose to include your labour separately in the formula, e.g. $30 per corner would be  cost * 3.0 + 30 

    • You could choose to include your labour as a separate charge using the Fitting category in the Price Codes file.

    • It really doesn’t matter what method you choose as long as your staff knows how to enter it at the time of sale on a work order.

Important: Costs, markups, and retail prices used in the above instructions are for purposes of example only and NOT suggested pricing.

How to Price on a Work Order

  1. On your Work Order, go to Extra field and, from the drop-down menu, choose your Item.

  2. If you have set your pricing to charge for one corner, then put a quantity of  4  in the yellow Qty field.

  3. If you need to charge separately for labour, then use the Fitting drop-down menu to select your labour charge.

How to use Corners in your Purchase Orders

  1. When you create your Frame Order, select the Extra checkbox.

  2. Also select the Create Purchase Orders checkbox.

  3. The Corners then appear on both the Frame Order print out as well as the Purchase Order for the supplier.
