Find Screen Explained

Notable Search Fields

  • See also: Find Overview

Payment Status

  • You can search for Invoices that are Paid in Full, Deposit, Unpaid, Refund, or Void.

Line Items Section

Tag Field

  • Line items can be tagged upon creation and searched for in the Tag field. When printed on an Invoice, tagged items are grouped together and a subtotal footer is calculated for each tag used.

Timestamp Field

  • When a Work Order or Product is added to an Invoice, FrameReady records the date and time in a single timestamp field. A timestamp field includes both the date and time.

  • Filemaker Pro allows you to relax a bit when searching timestamp fields. For example:

 Sat 2018 

- All Saturdays in 2018

 Mon..Fri 2018 8..10 PM 

- Weekdays between 8 and 10 o'clock in the morning

 12/2018 {4..6} PM 

- The month of December, 2018 from four in the afternoon until seven o'clock in the evening

 AM 

- The morning (from midnight unttl noon)

 Sat..Sun 2018 AM 

- Weekend mornings for 2018

Morning Button

  • Inserts  */*/* {1..11}:*:* AM  into the Timestamp field.

  • This searches for any values between 1 AM and 11:59 AM.

Afternoon Button

  • Inserts  */*/* {12..5}:*:* PM  into the Timestamp field.

  • This searches for any values between 12:00 PM and 5:59 PM.

Evening Button

  • Inserts  */*/* {6..12}:*:* PM  into the Timestamp field.

  • This searches for any values between 6 PM and 12:59 PM.

Payment Lines Section

Time Field

  • When you take a payment on an Invoice, FrameReady records the date and time (in separate fields) of the transaction.

  • Filemaker Pro allows you to relax a bit when searching timestamp fields. For example:

 3 PM 

- from 3:00:00 PM until 3:59:59 PM

 8 AM..8 PM 

- from 8:00 in the morning until 1 second before 9:00

 AM 

- the morning (from midnight until noon)

Date Field (Payment Lines Section)

  • When you take a payment on an Invoice, FrameReady records the date and time (in separate fields) of the transaction.

  • Filemaker Pro allows you to relax a bit when searching date fields. For example:

 12/2018 

- December, 2018

 Fri 

- Fridays

 {6..8} 2018 

- June, July and August of 2018

 7/2014..7/2018 

- from July to July

Morning Button

  • This searches for any values between 1 AM and 11:59 AM.

Afternoon Button

  • Inserts  {12..5}:*:* PM  into the Timestamp field.

  • This searches for any values between 12:00 PM and 5:59 PM.

Evening Button

  • Inserts  {6..12}:*:* PM  into the Timestamp field.
