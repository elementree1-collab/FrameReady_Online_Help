Update Glass Pricing

Price Codes > Glazing Sidebar Button

Although moulding, matboard, and fabric items are automatically updated through your FrameReady Subscription, glass items are not.

Glass prices need to be updated manually. However, since there are fewer options than matboard or moulding, the process is fairly quick and easy.

How to Update the Wholesale Cost for Glass

Regardless of how you price your glass, these two things should be done to update your wholesale cost on each glass records.

1) Enter the wholesale cost of one lite into the blue field for Cost

  • The cost per lite should be based on the Dimensions field. If left blank, then FrameReady assumes the dimensions are 32×40.

  • You can calculate the price per lite by dividing the box price by the number of lites per box.

  • If you receive a discount from the supplier, then enter the discount percentage as a decimal in the Discount field in the Auto Inv tab.

Unless you are competing on price (as this transfers the discount to the customer without their awareness), do not include the discount in your calculation of wholesale cost.

  • If you wish to pass on your savings to your customer, then use the discount field on the Work Order (under Glazing/Fabric) to show the discount.

2) Enter today’s date in the Validation Date field

  • This identifies when you last updated your wholesale costs and/or retail prices.

How Glass Pricing Changes Affect the Retail Price

If You Price By Markup

  • If you are pricing your glass with markups, then any change in your cost automatically increases or decreases the retail price.

  • Cost x Markup (+ Set Price) = Retail Price

If You Price By Code Tables

  • If you are pricing by Code Tables, then the new wholesale cost will not change the retail price.

  • You can adjust your retail price by clicking the Code Tables button, then clicking on the Adjust by % button, to increase (or decrease) your retail prices by a percentage.

If You Price By Individual Lite Size (for inventory control)

  • Changing the wholesale costs will not affect the retail price.

  • You will need to open the Glazing Pricing tab, then open the Set Price tab, and type in the new retail price.

  • Update your costs as often as necessary and check your retail prices to ensure your profit margin.
