Reports from Work Orders

The following documents and reports can be printed from within Work Orders and/or the Main Menu:

There are three beneficial Work Order related reports:

Sales Report by Category

  • This report shows the different types of artwork that are being framed and the amount of revenue brought in for each type. It also shows the amount discounted and the average price per UI.

Sales Report by Employee

  • This report shows the same information as above but it is sorted by who took the order.

Sales Report by Designer

  • This report shows the same information as the Category report but sorted by the Designer field
