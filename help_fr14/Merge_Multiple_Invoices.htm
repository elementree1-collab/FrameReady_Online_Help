Merge Multiple Invoices

Combine two or more Invoices within FrameReady by removing all their line items and adding them back onto the final Invoice.

Before You Begin...

How to Merge Multiple Invoices

The first step is to "remove and unpost" the Work Order(s) from the Invoice(s) that you don't want to keep. 

Remove Line Items from an Invoice and Discard

  1. Locate any one of the customer's Invoices in question.

  2. Click the dollar amount in the Total Due field (this is actually a button).



    All the unpaid Invoices for that customer appear, followed by a summary line for subtotal, savings, taxes, payments and current balance.

  3. Choose an Invoice without any payments.

  4. Click the purple Data Entry button.

  5. If the Invoice date is not from the current month, then click the Allow this Invoice to be Modified button.

  6. Write down the line item numbers, including Work Order numbers -- you will need these numbers later.

  7. Click the X (in the teal square) to delete the line item that you no longer want posted on this Invoice.

    If prompted to unpost the Work Order before deleting, then choose Yes.

  8. When all the line items have been removed, click Done.

  9. Click Void (top left) to discard this Invoice. There should be no line items and no payments.

    Optionally add a note explaining why this Invoice is being voided, e.g. "merging with invoice #number".

  10. Click the List View button to return to the list of Invoices.

  11. Repeat steps 1-10 as needed.

Add Line Items to an Invoice

The second step is to add the recently-unposted Work Orders onto the Invoice that you wish to keep.

  1. Locate the Invoice that you want to add items to. Click the purple Data Entry button.

  2. If the Invoice date is not from the current month, then click the Allow this Invoice to be Modified button.

  3. Click the magnifying glass icon  and enter the item or Work Order number in the Item Number field.



    Click the Search button.

  4. Repeat steps 1-3 for the remaining items.

  5. When the Invoice contains all the line items you need, Enter a Payment as normal.
