Add your own Mirrors

Main Menu > Price Codes

Use this method to add your own mirrors into FrameReady.

How to Add Mirrors into your Price Codes

  1. On the Main Menu, in the Price Codes section, click the New Price Code Item button.

  2. The New Price Code Item window appears.

  3. Choose Glazing (or Glass in earlier versions).

  4. Enter an Item name or number.

Caution: If you have two items with the same item number, then FrameReady use the pricing of the first item on the Work Order. This is why you cannot have any duplicates!

  1. Enter the wholesale cost in the blue Cost field.

  2. In the Dimensions field, choose  12 x 12  .

  3. Choose a Supplier (the company who manufactures the product and may not necessarily be the vendor from whom you order it).

    To include this item in Purchase Orders, set the Order From field.

  4. Open the Glass Pricing tab.

  5. Enter your Markup, e.g.  2.5  .

  6. To the right of the Markup field, select the Square Foot radio button.

  7. Optionally, add a Set Price to cover cutting, shipping, or handling fees.

  8. Create a test Work Order, add the item into the Glazing/Fabric field, and confirm the pricing.

Any costs, markups, and retail prices used in the above instructions are for purposes of example only and not suggested pricing.
