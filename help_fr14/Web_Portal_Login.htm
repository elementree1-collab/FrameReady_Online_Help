Web Portal

Main Menu > Setup Data > Integrations 

FrameReady offers a web portal for your customers to Log in and see their progress on Work Orders, leave notes and notifications for your office on Jobs, view their information in list and detail view and much more.

Web Portal Login & Security

  • After the Web Portal files are hosted on your FileMaker Server, the login page can be accessed by going to "/Login.php"

  • When first visiting the Web Portal, it defaults to display a login screen with a username and password field.

  • You can set the Username and Password fields, per customer, in their Contact record in FrameReady. Locate the customer in the Contact file and open the Work Orders tab.

 

  • The Web Portal is protected by an IP blocking security measure. If someone tries to log in with an incorrect username or password more than 5 (five) times, then their IP address is blocked from accessing the Web Portal for 5 (five) hours. This helps to prevent bots from hacking into a customers details through the portal.

  • Also on the Log In screen, is a “Forgot your password” feature that customers can click and enter their username to retrieve their password to the Web Portal. When they enter their username and click Submit, the system will send an email to the email address that is connected to their Contact record in FrameReady containing their password to the portal.

  • When you have logged into the Web Portal using the customers username and password, it will direct to the Dashboard page of the web portal ‘/Dashboard.php’. This page has several buttons to direct users to the different modules. If you have turned off any of the modules in the web portal preferences, those buttons will not show up on this screen.

  • Click on any of the buttons to use the associated module.

  • Click the Invoices or Work Orders button for a list view or Invoices or Work Orders.

  • Click the Contact Information for a detail view of the customer's Contact Information. Users can also edit their information.

  • Lastly, there is a Logout button at the top right.
