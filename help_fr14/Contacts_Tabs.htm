Contacts Tabs

Main Menu > Contacts

Each colored tab in the lower half of the Contacts form view takes you to a detail screen presenting helpful information concerning this contact or customer.

  • You can store additional data such as: birthdays, tax exemption information, referrals, shipping address, preferred artists, and in-store credits.

  • Work Orders and Invoices can be easily accessed also.

Tabs in the Contacts File

  • Keywords Tab

  • Terms Tab

  • Shipping Tab

  • Referrals Tab

  • Date/Notes Tab

  • Group/Details Tab

  • Artists Tab

  • Invoices/Rewards Tab

  • Invoice Details Tab

  • Receive Payment Tab

  • Credits Tab

  • Work Orders Tab
