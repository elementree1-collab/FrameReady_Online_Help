Square Integration

Connect FrameReady to Square

Main Menu > Setup Data > Integrations 

FrameReady is integrated with Square and offers you the option to process credit card transactions, refunds, and voids using your Square account and your Square Terminal (which can be purchased from the Square website).

  • Connect FrameReady to your Square account, register your terminal, and then you are all set to process payments with Square, inside of FrameReady!

  • You will need a Square Terminal which can purchased from the Square website. Other Square devices are not supported.

  • https://squareup.com/us/en/hardware/terminal

  • Once you have your terminal, you connect your Square account with FrameReady, register your terminal and then you are all set to process payments with Square inside of FrameReady.

  • Note: the Square Terminal cannot be used to place transactions while it is logged into FrameReady. Instead, enter the transaction into FrameReady as a new Invoice.

Requirements

  • Requires a Square Terminal purchased from the Square website.

  • Other Square devices are not supported.

  • Compatible with Windows or Mac.

  • Requires an internet connection.

  • Requires FrameReady 13 and FileMaker Pro 19.4 or greater.
