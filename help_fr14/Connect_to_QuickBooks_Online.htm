Connect to QuickBooks Online

Use these steps to setup your QuickBooks integration with FrameReady.

How to Connect to your QuickBooks Online Account

Tip: This is a once-a-year procedure.

  1. Log in as level4.

  2. On the Main Menu, click the Setup Data icon.

  3. Open the Fiscal tab.

  4. Click the Open QuickBooks Integration Settings button.

  5. The Main QuickBooks Integration window appears.

  6. Click the green Connect to QuickBooks button.

  7. In the QuickBooks Sign in form, enter your QuickBooks Online credentials and click the green Sign in button.

  8. If QuickBooks prompts you to choose which company to connect to, then click the appropriate company.

  9. If QuickBooks requests you to authorize FrameReadyQB to access your QuickBooks Online data, then click the green Connect button.

  10. Windows users: copy the Authentication Code and then click Scripts > QB Online - Authentication - Manual Finish for Windows Users. In the dialogue that appears, paste in the Authentication Code and click OK.

  11. You should receive a "FrameReady is now authorized..." message. Click the blue Complete button (top right).

  12. The Authentication Code and Company ID fields are populated on Main Integration Settings.

    Quickbooks requires the application be re-authenticated once a year, so the "Connection Expires" date will display the date by which this account connection process needs to be redone.

    Quickbooks authentication is now complete and FrameReady is connected to your Quickbooks Online account.
