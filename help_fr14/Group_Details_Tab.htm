Group Details Tab

Main Menu > Contacts > Group/Details tab

Tip: The Group/Details tab is located at the middle bottom of the Contacts file.

  • Set yourself above your competition with your customer service and design your marketing campaigns using this data.

  • Track customer birthdays, anniversary’s, children’s names, spouses, pets, hobbies or any other grouping you wish using the Group/Details field.

Group/Details Tab Explained

Group Drop-down

  • A drop-down list of available Groups. Choose Edit... menu option to add a new one.

Details Field

  • A pop-up calendar; preset to the current date; automatically added if a Note is entered first.

Delete Button

  • Empties the contents of the entire row on which the button appears.

How to Add a Group and Enter the Details

  1. Click in the Group field and choose an option.

    Or click Edit… menu option to add a new one.

  2. Type in the Details in the next field, i.e. spouse’s name.

  3. In future, search for anything in these fields using Find button.

Tip: This information may also be entered from the New Contact Enter Information screen.
