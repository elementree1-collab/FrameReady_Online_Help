Sell Supplies as Retail Items

Main Menu > Invoices > Line Item Entry Screen

How to Sell a Supply Item on an Invoice

First add a Retail Price to the Price Code Item

  1. If you haven’t already entered a retail price for the Supply item in the Retail Pricing tab, then do so.

    The Retail Pricing tab is for calculating a price when the item is sold on an Invoice.

  2. Open the Retail Pricing tab.

  3. Enter a markup in the Unit Markup field, e.g.  2 

  4. If the item is sold in a quantity greater than 1, then enter the quantity in the Unit Qty field.

  5. If the item is Tax Exempt, then check the appropriate box(es).

Then, to sell it on an Invoice

  1. Click the Data Entry button on your Invoice.

  2. Type in the moulding/matboard number with it’s suffix in the Line Item entry.

  3. Enter number of pieces being sold in Qty field.
