Purchase Orders List View

Main Menu > Purchase Orders

Top Nav Section Explained

Find Back Order Button

  • Searches all POs for line items that have marked as "Back Order."

New Purchase Order Button

  • Creates a new blank order; the PO number and date are filled automatically.

  • Item Numbers that match items in the Price Codes or Products file auto-fill related fields (Description, Price, etc.).

Merge 2 POs Together Button

  • Allows you to move the line items from an existing PO to another existing PO. 

  • You must know the PO numbers for both before using.

List View Explained

Marked

  • The Marked box can be used to identify any group of records which can be searched.

PO #

  • Generated by FrameReady.

Date

  • The creation date of the Purchase Order record.

Vendor

  • The Company name of the Contact record assigned to the Purchase Order.

Subtotal

  • The PO total before taxes.

Tax1, Tax2

  • The PO total before taxes.

Total

  • The combined subtotal and taxes.

Status

  • NotPlaced, Placed, or Received.

Terms

  • A pop-up menu to select from; Net, Net/30, COD.

  • Use the Edit… feature at the bottom to modify the list.

Footer Area

  • Displays record count and found set.

  • Displays Subtotal, Tax, and Total calcuation (level3 and level4 only).
