Set up Business Info

Main Menu > Setup Data button > Info tab

Enter information pertaining to basic areas of your business.

Setup Data - Info tab Explained

Business Name Field

  • Enter your Business Name on one or two lines. This is how it will appear in all printed documentation if a Logo is not used.

Tip: You can format text (font, size and color) from Menu Bar > Format or select some text and right-click.

Business Address Field

  • Enter your Business Address on two lines: street address on first line, the remainder on the second.

Shipping Address Field

  • Enter your Shipping Address only if it is different from the above Business Address. Otherwise, keep it empty.

Phone 1 and Phone 2 Fields

  • Enter your Phone number. You may use Phone 2 for fax.

Email Field

  • Enter your business email address. It will appear on printed customer documents.

Website Field

  • Enter your business website address.

  • Appears on printed customer documents.

  • Start with "www" and do not include "http://"

Social Media Field

  • Enter your social media links, e.g.  www.facebook.com/your-fan-page 

Imperial / Metric Radio Button

New in FrameReady 12 - support for both imperial and metric measurements and calculations.

  • Select the unit of measurement. This is a required field.

  • If left empty, then FrameReady cannot display the measurement data for Mat Margins, White Space, Reveals, etc.

Headings Field

  • Select the appropriate headings for your Country, Territory and Postal/zip code.

  • Sales taxes are calculated based on the selection in the Country field (not the Metric or Imperial radio buttons). 

    • If the country is: USA, UK or Canada, then the taxes are added to the subtotal. ie $10 + 1.30 = $11.30

    • If the country is AUS, then the tax is NOT added to the subtotal. ie $10 + 1.30 = $10

  • Click Done to return to the Main Menu, or select another tab.
