Return a Product to Inventory after a Refund

How to return a retail product to your inventory after a refund has been issued.

How to Return a Product to Inventory after a Refund

  1. Create a New Invoice.

  2. Enter the customer’s name.

  3. Use the Magnifying glass icon to enter the Product or Work Order number of the returned item in the line entry section of the screen.

    This will help to locate all components of the Work Order and allow the retail product to be updated in the Product file. If no price appears, then enter it in the Unit Price field.

  4. Enter the Quantity as a negative number, e.g.  -1 

  5. Click the Enter a Payment button.

    The dollar amount will be negative.

  6. Click in the Tender field and choose the refund you used, for example:

    Refund - Store Credit
    Refund - Cash
    Refund - Amex
    Refund - MC
    Refund - VISA

  7. Click Done.

  8. Optionally, enter an explanation for the refund in the Invoice Note field (lower left corner of the Invoice screen).

How to Return a L/E Print to Inventory

If the item has been removed from inventory because the box in the Product file was checked “Delete limited edition items when sold”, then you must create a new record in the Product file.

If you wish to have the option of being able to return LE prints to inventory, then make sure that checkbox remains unchecked. The checkbox is located here: Products > Artwork tab, beside the Edition #

  1. Go to Invoice file and click New Invoice.

  2. Enter the Product or Work Order number of the returned item in the line entry.

    You may use the Search Item button to locate the retail item.

  3. Enter the Quantity as a negative number, e.g.  -1  

    If no price appears, then enter it in the Unit Price field.

  4. Click the Enter a Payment button.

    The amount will be negative.

  5. Click in the Tender field and choose the refund you used, for example:

    Refund - Store Credit
    Refund - Cash
    Refund - Amex
    Refund - MC
    Refund - VISA

  6. Click Done.
