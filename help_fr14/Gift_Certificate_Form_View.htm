Gift Certificate / Store Credit Form View Explained

Name, Company, Phone Buttons

  • Each Gift Certificate / Store Credit must be assigned to a Contact. Gift Cards are identified as Walk In, as the card may be given to anyone (it is not linked to the customer who purchased it).

  • Select the customer purchasing this certificate by using the Find By Name, By Company or By Phone buttons.

  • If the customer is not in your database then add them by clicking New Customer sidebar button.

ID#

  • The Contact ID number. This unique number links customer information throughout the files and is automatically assigned.

Serial Number Field

  • A pre-filled with a sequential serial number.

  • Can be overwritten to reflect your needs; must not contain duplicate numbers!

Date Field

  • Auto-filled with today’s date.

Redeemed Radio Buttons

  • Automatically marked as redeemed ‘No‘.

Category Drop-Down

  • Options for Gift Certificate, Gift Card, Store Credit, Coupon.

Certificate Wording Field

  • As requested by your customer in the To and From fields.

  • Have staff identify themselves as the issuer of store credit.

Amount Field

  • The dollar Amount (required) of the gift certificate, gift card or store credit.

By Line Drop-down

  • The By Line (optional).

  • Select from a drop-down menu of pre-entered options or edit the list to offer different choices in Edit… or add a one time line by using Other…

When Printing Panel

New in FrameReady 11 -- Print Options have been moved to the Main Menu, under the Option tab for the Gift Certificate section.

  • Pause on Preview: Yes, No, Don't Preview

  • Show Print Dialog box: Yes, No

Gift Card Use Only Section

Add Credit Button

  • Add credit to the current gift card.

  • The Amount value adjusts accordingly.

Remove Remove Button

  • Remove credit from the current gift card.

  • The Amount value adjusts accordingly.

New Gift Card Override Button

  • Creates a new Gift Card without creating an Invoice.

  • Triggers a dialog requesting a Card Number and the dollar Amount.

Gift Card Table

  • FrameReady records all gift card activity, the Invoice number and credit/debit values for the selected card.

  • These fields are not modifiable.
