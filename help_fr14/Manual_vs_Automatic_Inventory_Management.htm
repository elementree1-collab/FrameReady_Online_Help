Inventory

Manual vs Automatic Inventory Management

The inventory fields for framing materials are stored in the Price Codes file but can be viewed and/or modified from the Work Order file. Inventory of framing materials can be handled by one of two methods: manual or automatic.

  • Tracking inventory for custom framing is one of those procedures that either works or it doesn’t, depending on the commitment level of the person in charge. It requires a rigorous discipline of checking inventory in and out. Fortunately, FrameReady can make the task a lot easier. Some of the benefits include: reducing time spent searching for stock, the taking of a physical inventory, and being able to tell the customer with a rush order, “Yes, we have it in stock” while still in the work order screen.

Pros & Cons of using Manual Inventory

See also: Manual Moulding Inventory

The Plus Side

  • This method is totally accurate (or as accurate as you want it to be). The moulding inventory can show the exact footage for each stick, e.g. 2 – 10ft lengths, 1 – 8ft length, 1 – 5.5ft length, etc.

  • When you use a piece or full stick, it is up to you to remove it from inventory. This can be done right from the Work Order Frame1 – Frame5 detail screens (e.g. on a back room computer when the cutting is completed). Any changes made there also change the related record in the Price Codes file.

  • You can chose not to return partial pieces to inventory and only record full lengths or lengths larger than 5 feet, for instance.

The Negative Side

  • This method is more time consuming.

Pros & Cons of using Automatic Inventory

See also: Automatic Inventory Updates

The Plus Side

  • It is fast and easy.

  • FrameReady calculates the moulding footage used on a Work Order, removes the required footage from inventory, identifies the supplier you have selected to order and, when you generate a Purchase Order, enters the amount onto it.

  • If you use the automatic update system, then a report can be printed for all Price Code items sorted by Location or Group.

The Negative Side

  • FrameReady won’t know if there was a flaw in the material and you actually used more than required.

  • Or if an error is made in cutting and more material needs to be taken from inventory. Also, after removing 8 feet from a 10 foot length of moulding the computer will leave 2 feet in stock while in fact it may have been discarded.

  • Only the total footage is shown on the inventory screen. You must set up this option using the Main Menu > Work Orders Options screen.
