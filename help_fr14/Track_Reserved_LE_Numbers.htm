Track Reserved LE Numbers

Main Menu > Contacts

Sometimes customers reserve certain numbers of Limited Editions. This is a way to track them.

How to Print Reserved Limited Edition Numbers

  1. Locate the customer's record in the Contacts file (form view).

  2. Open the Artist tab.

  3. Click the Print Reserve button.

  4. The print preview appears. Click Continue to print.
