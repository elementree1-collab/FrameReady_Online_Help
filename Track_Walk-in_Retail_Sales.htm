Track Walk-in Retail Sales

Sometimes a customer does not want to leave their name or information when making a small purchase.

  • You have the choice of whether or not you wish to require a customer name on an invoice.

  • If no name is required on an Invoice, e.g. for small item purchase, tourist, etc., but you want to track these sales:

How to Track Walk-in Sales on Invoices

  1. Go to Contacts file.

  2. Create a record by clicking the New Contact button in top right corner.

  3. Enter 'Walk-in' in either First or Last name fields or, better yet, enter 'Valued Customer' into the first name field or 'Happy Tourist' in the first name field.

  4. When you go to create a new Invoice, enter this 'customer' in the customer field.

    This will make it easy to find and all invoices which do not have a customer name on it. You will also be able to track total sales of small items sold to customers before they become 'regulars'. You will also able to track which items are selling. What is in the display window that is drawing them in?
