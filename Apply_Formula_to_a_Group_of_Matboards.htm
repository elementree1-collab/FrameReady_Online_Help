How to Apply Your own Formula to a Group of Matboards 

To apply your new pricing formula:

  1. Go to Price Codes > Matboard sidebar button.

  2. If you wish to work with a subset of matboards, then first perform a find. When you have your found set, switch to form view and continue to step 3.

  3. Open the blue Matboard Pricing tab.

  4. Click the Apply This Formula to a Group of Records button.

  5. A dialog box appears.

  6. This is where you can apply a formula to a specific vendor or to a specific size.

    The formula appears in the far left column labelled Markup. Other columns include Set Price, Minimum Price and Code.

  7. The parent Group, e.g. Matboard, is pre-chosen.

  8. Optionally, choose a Supplier.

    Leave this blank to apply to all suppliers.

  9. Optionally, choose Dimensions.

    This is where you can apply the formula to a specific size, such as, 32 x 40.

    Leave blank to apply to all records regardless of dimensions.

  10. Optionally, choose Omit items where Category equals.

    Leave this blank to apply to all records regardless of dimensions.

  11. The Combine Formula and Code Pricing checkbox is used in special circumstances where neither a formula nor a Code Table will work but a combination of the two will.

  12. Click Apply.

    FrameReady finds all records matching the selected criteria and replaces the Markup and Set Price fields.

  13. Click Apply to Selected Criteria.

    FrameReady finds all records matching the selected criteria and replaces the Markup and Set Price fields.
