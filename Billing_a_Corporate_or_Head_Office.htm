Billing a Corporate or Head Office

This work-around allows you to take an order for a customer and then bill a corporate or head office.

How to Bill an Invoice to a Corporate or Head Office

  1. Locate the Contact record in question.

  2. In the Note field, enter the Contact ID number that the individual's purchases should be "Billed To:"

  3. Then, when the Invoice is created: in the Invoice Note field, enter the Name and Customer ID # of who made the purchase.

  4. Next, update the contact to the billing contact's ID # (as seen in the Cust Note field).

  5. Click the purple Data Entry button.

  6. In the Line Item screen, place your cursor in the ID# field.

  7. Type in the correct Customer ID number (as found in the Customer Note field).

  8. Click outside the field to commit the change.

  9. Click the Done button.

  10. Over time, you will have several invoices for the corporate or head office. You can easily find those Invoices by entering the billing contact's Customer ID # and/or also adding a date range.

  11. You can email the individual Invoices to them if they need to see what was purchased and/or you can send a statement to them showing the list of Invoices and amount due.
