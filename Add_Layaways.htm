Add Layaways

Main Menu > Invoices > New Invoice

My customer wishes to make layaway payments on a purchase. I don’t want to put it through my books until the purchase has been paid in full.

How to Handle Lay-aways

Set up the Layaway

  1. On the Main Menu, in the Invoices section, click the New Invoice button.

  2. Click the magnifying glass icon (left of the first line item) and enter the item. Click Search or Show All and select the correct item.

  3. Click in the Category field and change it to  Layaway  . If not listed, click the cursor into the field and type it in.

  4. Take note of the balance, including the tax. Change the Unit Price field to this total.

  5. Put an X in the tax exemption field/s to the far right.

  6. Click the Recalculate button.

    The balance should now be the same as before, but with no taxes showing.

  7. Enter a layaway installment using the Enter A Payment button.

    Keep entering installments using this method until the balance is zero.

  8. Take note of the Invoice # (top right).

For the Final Payment

  1. When the Invoice has been paid in full, then create a New Invoice.

  2. Type “Layaway on #…” into the Description field.

    Do not enter the item again using the Search Item button because this remove it from your inventory again and you don’t want that.

  3. Enter the before-tax Unit Price and then click the Recalculate button.

  4. Click the Enter a Payment button.

    When the Receipts dialog box appears, enter ‘Layaway Credit’ into the Tender field. If it is not already on the pop-up menu, then scroll to the bottom enter it in the Edit.… mode.

  5. Enter the original layaway Invoice # into the Reference field.

  6. Click Done or Done Print Invoice.
