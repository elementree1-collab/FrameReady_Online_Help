How to Target Referrals with a Physical Thank You Letter

Let’s suppose you want to find all the customers who made a referral from January 1st to March 31st and send them a thank you letter. Do the following:

  1. Click the Contacts > Find button.

  2. Click in the Date of Referrals field and type your date range, e.g. 1/1/2018…3/31/2018.

    The format is      DD/MM/YYYY...DD/MM/YYY 

  3. Click Perform Find.

    A list of all the customers who made a referral during that period appears.

  4. Choose a record to view; it does not matter which.

  5. Click the Print Bulk Letters sidebar button.

    The Sort Records dialog box appears.

  6. If you wish, you may change the sort order of the records. Click Sort or Cancel.

    The screen changes to a letterhead with the first customer’s name and address inserted in the appropriate place.

  7. From the drop-down list, click on the letter template “Thank you – Referral”.

  8. Make any changes to the text of your letter. The same letter will go to everyone on the list with their name and address. Use the Spelling button to perform a spell check on your letter before printing.

Tip: To tab within the body of your letter, hold the down Option (Mac) or Ctrl (Windows) and then press Tab .

  1. When ready, click the Print button.

    A print preview of the document appears.

  2. Click Continue.

    The print dialog box appears.

  3. Click Print (Mac) or OK (Windows).

    The screen returns to the Contacts file.

Note: The customer's first name (or the title and last name) is automatically inserted after the salutation “Dear”. If the letter is addressed to a company, to the attention of someone, then the name of the person in the Attention field is inserted in the salutation. If the Attention and the name fields are blank, then the salutation will read as “Dear Sir/Madam” Using the arrow buttons you can scroll through your records to see how names and addresses will appear on the letter.

Tip: Using the same model as printing a referral letter you can also print letters based on a customer’s last purchase. Follow the steps above but instead of searching the Date of Referral field use the Last Purchase field.
