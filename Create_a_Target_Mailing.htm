Create a Target Mailing

Main Menu > Contacts

This feature makes it easy to send a direct mailing to a subset of customers who share similar keywords.

How to Target a Mailing by Keyword

  1. On the Main Menu, in the Contacts section, click the Find Contact button.

  2. In the Customer Preferences section, tick your keyword checkboxes.

  3. Click Perform Find.

    A list view with your search results appears.

  4. Click the Print Bulk Letters button.

  5. The Sort Records dialog box appears.

  6. Select your sort order; click items and click Move or Clear. When ready, click Sort.

    If you do not want the names sorted, then simply click Cancel.

    The screen changes to a letterhead with the first customer’s name and address inserted in the appropriate place.

  7. Select the letter you wish to use or create a new one by clicking on the New Letter button on the menu palette.

  8. Type in the text of your letter. Use the Spelling button to perform a spell check on your letter before printing.

Tip: To tab within the body of your letter, hold the down Option (Mac) or Ctrl (Windows) and then press the Tabkey.

  1. When ready, click the Print button.

    A preview of your letter appears on the screen.

  2. Click the Continue button.

    A Print dialog box appears.

  3. Click Print (Mac) or OK (Windows).

How to Target a Mailing by Artist

When you create a Work Order or Invoice and add an Artist to a customer’s record, you can later send direct mailings to all customers who like that particular artist.

How to Compose and Print a Personal Letter

  1. Go to Main Menu > Contacts file > Find button.

  2. In the Artists field, select the artist for the target mailing using the drop-down list.

Tip: If you wish to select several artists for this mailing, then click Add New Request from the Requests menu after selecting your first artist.

Another request screen will appear and you can select another artist from the drop-down list. Repeat for as many artists as you wish.

  1. Click Perform Find.

    A list view appears of all the customers who at one time purchased artwork by the selected artist(s).

  2. Click the Print Bulk Letters sidebar button.

    The Sort dialog box appears.

  3. In the Sort Records dialog box, select your sort order. Click Sort. If you do not want the names sorted, click Cancel.

    The screen changes to a letterhead with the first customer’s name and address inserted in the appropriate place.

  4. Select the letter you wish to use or create a new one by clicking on the New Letter button on the menu palette.

  5. Type in the text of your letter; use the Spelling button to perform a spell check on your letter before printing.

Tip: To tab within the body of your letter, hold the down Option (Mac) or Ctrl (Windows) and then press the Tab key.

  1. When ready, click the Print button.

    A print preview appears.

  2. Click Continue.

    The print dialog box appears.

  3. Click Print (Mac) or OK (Windows).

    The screen returns to the Contacts file.

How to Target Referrals with a Thank You Letter

One of the features in FrameReady is the ability to track referrals. When a new customer comes through your door, they often do so on the basis of a referral from a friend or family member. Tracking those referrals is profitable and easy each time you create a new customer record. Let’s suppose you want to find all the customers who made a referral from January 1st to March 31st and send them a thank you letter. Do the following:

  1. Go to Contacts file > Find.

  2. Click in the Date of Referrals field and type your date range. e.g. 1/1/2012…3/31/2012.

  3. Click Perform Find.

    A list appears of all the customers who made a referral during that period.

  4. Click Print Bulk Letters side bar button.

    A sort dialog box appears.

  5. If you wish to change the sort order of the records, then click Sort, otherwise click Done.

    The screen changes to a letterhead with the first customer’s name and address inserted in the appropriate place.

  6. From the drop-down list, click on the letter template “Thank you – Referral”.

  7. Make any changes to the text of your letter. The same letter will go to everyone on the list with their name and address. Use the Spelling button to perform a spell check on your letter before printing.

    To tab within the body of your letter, hold the down Option key (Mac) or Ctrl key (Windows) and then press the Tab key.

  8. When ready, click the Print button.

    A preview of how your letter will look will appear on the screen.

  9. Click Continue.

    The Print dialog box appears.

  10. Click Print (Mac) or OK (Windows).

    The screen returns to the Contacts file.

Note: The customer’s first name (or the title and last name) is automatically inserted after the salutation “Dear”. If the letter is addressed to a company, to the attention of someone, then the name of the person in the Attention field is inserted in the salutation. If the Attention and the name fields are blank, then the salutation will read as “Dear Sir/Madam” Using the arrow buttons you can scroll through your records to see how names and addresses will appear on the letter.

Tip: Using the same model as printing a referral letter you can also print letters based on a customer’s last purchase. Follow the steps above but instead of searching the Date of Referral field use the Last Purchase field.
