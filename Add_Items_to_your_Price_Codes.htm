Add Items to your Price Codes

Main Menu > Price Codes

Customizing the Price Codes is one of the most important things to do when first setting up your program. Time and effort spent here will be greatly rewarded when designing and pricing a custom frame.

  • One of the first steps in setting up your program is to make sure you have the drop-down lists in the Work Order file set up to meet your shop's needs. While the framing components appear in the Work Order screen, the data is entered and changed in the Price Codes file.

Group Designations

  • The Price Codes file is organized based on Groups, represented by side bar buttons.

  • Each component that goes into the assembly of a custom picture frame must be categorized into a Group.

  • In setting up your program, you must determine what item fits into what group. Some are obvious, some are not, e.g. you need to decide whether to place "shadow box treatment" under the Mounting group (Mount/Stretch in Work Order file) or the Extra group. It is a matter of placing the component, design feature or procedure in the group that you think best fits your way of pricing and organizing. 

  • Some groups offer different methods of pricing.

    • Mat Design, Hardware, Extra and Fitting are the same.

    • Matboard, Mounting and Glass have the same pricing options.

  • Knowing how you want to price an item, may determine into which Group it should be placed.

  • FrameReady comes preloaded with a few items in each group as a starting point. During the initial set up of your program, it is your job to add, delete, or rename these items according to your needs.

  • Warning: Do not change the item numbers on moulding, matboard and fabric as this will cause them to be identified as discontinued.

  • Then you must set up the pricing for each item in the group in order to make the pricing in the Work Order come out the way you want.

  • When you click in the field for each component on the Work Order, you will see the items for the specific group as they are listed in the Price Codes file.

Available Group Designations

  • Moulding (Wood, Metal, Plastic, Liner, Fillet, Extender)

  • Matboard

  • Fabric

  • Mat Design

  • Mounting

  • Hardware

  • Glass (plexi, mirrors, etc..)

  • Extra

  • Fitting

  • Multi-Angle

  • Supplies (not in Lite version)

  • Clicking on one of these side bar buttons will find all records for that group.

Note: The screen always displays the form view. You can see how many records belong to the group, where it says, 1 of ####, No of Records ####.

How to Work with Price Code Items

FrameReady comes preloaded with a few items in each group as a starting point. During the initial set up of your program, it is your job to add, delete, or rename these items according to your needs.

  • One of the first steps in setting up your program is to make sure you have the drop-down lists in the Work Order file set up to meet your shop's needs. The framing components appear in the Work Order screen but the data is entered/changed in the Price Codes file.

  • For the groups: Mat Design, Mounting, Hardware, Glass, Extra and Fitting you will need to:

    • View the default FrameReady items

    • Delete any you do not use

    • Rename unnecessary items to ones which you require, and lastly:

    • Add more items in each Group if necessary. If you forget one, you can always add it at any time.

  •  

  • All items which are currently listed in the group will appear on the screen in the order in which they were entered.

  • To identify an item you want to delete, click the Marked box (left of the item name). Then click the Delete Record (red) button at the top of the screen.

  • To change the name of an Item, click the item's name. The Item opens in Form View to reveal more information. Click in the Item field and write over or delete the name and enter what you want it to be. Then click the List View button (top centre) to go back to the list.

 

  • After customizing your items, and if you have Marked any records, then click the Delete Marked Records button. The items marked for deletion are listed: carefully check that only those items which you have marked are in the list--before you continue by clicking the Delete All button. (If someone else has marked items, then those will appear in your list as well, so you may need to Cancel if this is the case.)

  • After items are deleted, click the Form View button and then continue with the next group: Mounting, etc. until all Groups have been edited.

Tip: To see how your finished list will appear in the Work Order Screen, click on the Group, e.g. Mat Design, then click List View, then click the Item. The list will be sorted.) The list is sorted alphabetically, so you may want to group items together Eg. Vgroove - single; Vgroove - double, etc. Or, place number before the item to have them appear at the top of the list. Eg. 01 - Vgroove, 02 - Painted Panel, etc.

  • An example of how customizing the Fitting drop-down list would be adding an Increase Rabbet Depth item. If a frame job exceeds the rabbet depth of the chosen moulding, then choosing this item from your Fitting list on the Work Order would add a specified amount per UI to the frame job. This means you are not losing money on having to build up the back of the frame to accommodate the components.

To Add Items to Groups

  • To add new items, you can either duplicate a record or create a new one with the buttons in the top right corner. Use the New button or Duplicate Record button (available only in Form View).

  • In the Form View of the record, make the necessary changes to the item. As you type the changes, they are saved.

Tip: To see how your finished list will appear in the Work Order Screen, click on the group, (E.g. Mat Design, then click on List View, then click on the underlined word the “Item”. The list will be sorted.

Tip: The list is sorted alphabetically, so you may want to group items together E.g. Vgroove - single; Vgroove - double, etc. Or, place number before the item to have them appear at the top of the list. E.g. 01 - Vgroove, 02 - Painted Panel, etc.
