Product Suppliers

An overview of the supplier information section of the Products File.

Copy When Creating a New Record Checkbox 

  • This feature is a time saver when entering more than one product for the same supplier. If an X appears in the box, the supplier’s information will automatically be entered on the new record.

Supplier Field

  • The Supplier field displays the name of the company from whom the product will be ordered. A supplier must be identified in order to have FrameReady create a purchase order to replenish stock when the Threshold is reached.

ID# Field

  • All companies and customers listed in the Contacts file have an ID# which is generated by FrameReady when the record is created. Do not remove or change the identification number of any record. If it says Gallery, then it is your own gallery record.

  • Click the green X to remove the company and its information from the screen. It will not delete them from your database. You should clear the current company before selecting another to replace it.

New Vendor Button

  • The New Vendor button presents a dialog box, Enter Contact Name, to fill in.

  • Unlike the Price Codes file, the account number field is optional for Product vendors.

  • It is also recommended that you identify them as a supplier in Keywords tab. Suppliers can then be omitted or selected for promotional mailing.
