Items Revert to Old Prices

The Problem

  • Instead of the expected price change (by adjusting the Markup or Set Price), the old price comes up when added to a Work Order.

The Reason

  • There are two or more duplicate items in your Price Codes or Product file.

  • One item was changed but another, with the old price, is the one being added to the Work Order.

How to Remove Duplicate Items

The solution is to carefully remove duplicate items without impacting inventory counts.

  1. Perform a find in your Price Codes file.

  2. In the Item field, enter an exclamation mark:  ! 

Tip: This special character searches for duplicate data in the specified field.

  1. Optionally, select the vendor in the Supplier field, e.g. AMCI.

  2. Click the Perform Find button.

    A list view of results, all duplicate items, appears.

  3. In the list view screen, click the Item column heading.

    The list sorts by Item number, grouping them together.

  4. Examine the list to see which items have duplicates. Do take care in removing duplicates, as doing so may impact inventory. Examine the records and then Mark the records you don't want to keep

  5. When done marking records, click the Find Marked Records button.

  6. Then click the Flag As Discontinued button.

  7. Lastly, to remove the discontinued items, click the Delete All Discontinued Items button (or, if logged in as level4, choose Records > Delete Found Records...)

If You Do Not Track Inventory or Mark the Corner Sample Checkbox

  1. Log in as level4 .

  2. On the Main Menu, in the file menubar, click Perform and choose Restore Full Menus.

  3. Open the Price Codes file and click the Find button.

  4. Select your Supplier and click Perform Find.

  5. Go to the Records menu and select Delete Found Records.

  6. Click Delete All.

  7. Install the most recent update for your supplier.
