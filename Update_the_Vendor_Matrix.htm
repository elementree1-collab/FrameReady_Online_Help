Update the Vendor Matrix

Main Menu > Update Vendor Pricing button

Vendor-supplied matrixes are updated periodically by Adatasol Inc.

Tip: Matrix pricing can be updated from the Update Vendor Pricing screen only if your Annual Subscription is active.

  • See also: Working with the Canadian Matrix

How to Update Vendor Matrix Pricing

An active internet connection is required to perform this function.

  1. On the Main Menu, in the Price Codes section, click the Update Vendor Pricing button.

    The red circle to the right of this button indicates the number of updates available to you.

  2. Open the Vendor Codes tab.

  3. Click the underlined Update Canadian Vendor Codes text button.

  4. Follow the on-screen prompts.

While we try to make sure that all length, chop and join costs posted are accurate at all times, we cannot be responsible for typographical and other errors that may appear.
