Items Out On Consignment Report

Main Menu > Outbound Consignments

The Items Out On Consignment Report shows all retail products that are currently out of the shop on consignment to either decorators, auction, other galleries or on your sales truck.

Items Out on Consignment list

  1. Go to Outbound Consignments file, in form view.

  2. Click the Print All Items Out sidebar button.

  3. A print preview of the document appears.

  4. Click Continue.

  5. Select Yes or No.

Also available is a document listing all items out on consignment: click the Items Out On Consignment All Accounts sidebar button.

How to Print the Items Out On Consignment - All Accounts

This document lists all items out on consignment.

  1. Go to Outbound Consignments file form view 

  2. Click the Print Items Out All Accounts sidebar button.

  3. Click Continue to print.

How to Print the Items Out On Consignment - Image List

This Items Out on Consignment list shows all retail products with an image that are currently off your premises.

  1. Go to Outbound Consignments file form view.

  2. Click the Print Documents sidebar button.

  3. Select Image List.

  4. A print preview of the document appears.

  5. Click Continue and then select Print or Save as PDF.
