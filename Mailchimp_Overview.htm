Link your FrameReady Contacts to your Constant Contact Lists.

Market to your FrameReady customers using your Mailchimp account

FrameReady offers the option to import your Mailchimp Audiences (formerly Lists) and then subscribe/unsubscribe individual Contacts to one or several of your email marketing campaigns.

  • You can import your Audiences / Lists from Mailchimp into FrameReady.

  • You can assign Contacts from your FrameReady Contacts to one or multiple different Mailchimp Audiences / Lists.

  • You can also unsubscribe your Contacts from these Lists at any time in FrameReady.

  • These assignments are directly recorded in your Mailchimp account for ease of use. This makes managing your mailing lists a breeze since you can do it directly in FrameReady without having to do it in multiple places.
