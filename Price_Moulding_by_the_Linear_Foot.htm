Price Moulding by the Linear Foot

Main Menu > Price Codes > Moulding group

The Moulding group includes: Wood, Metal, Plastic (polystyrene), Liners, Fillets, and Extenders.

  • The moulding group pertains only to items usually bought and sold by the foot.

  • Bevelled Accents™ and Faux Fillets™ are also included in this group under Fillets.

Moulding Formula

  • The Moulding group is governed by this formula:

    Retail Price =  Required footage  x  Price per foot  +  Set Price 

Where:

  • Required footage = ( UI  + ( Moulding Width  x  4 )) ÷  6 

  • Required footage (metric) = ( United Centimetres  + (  Width  /  10  x  Width Multiplier ) x  4 )) /  50  x  10 . Then take the sum and round it up to the nearest whole number and /  10  x  Qty .

  • For example, (119 + ((40/ 10 x 1.25) x 4)) / 50 x 10) = 27.8 rounded up to 28 / 10 x 1 = 2.8 metres

  • Price per foot = this can be as simple as the price you want to charge or as complex as a Formula that includes a Cost (length, chop, or join), a Markup and an amount per foot (several examples are available)

  • Set Price = optional dollar amount added to the calculated price
