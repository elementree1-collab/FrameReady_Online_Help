Find your Inventory Items

There are a couple of different ways that you can find the items which have a quantity in the inventory field.

How to Find your Inventory Items

Option 1: Perform a Find

  • If you have an amount entered into the Qty field (which is the amount you have in inventory), then you can perform a find using an asterisk  *  in the Qty field to locate all moulding for which there is a stock of positive or negative value.

  • If you only want to find items with a positive number, then enter  >0  into the Qty field in your search. For the negative numbers, if you do not have any stock of that item, then delete the zero or negative number and it will not appear on any inventory reports either. After you have performed your Find, you can use the Inventory List button to easily view your inventory items.

Option 2: Use the Marked Checkbox

  • Use the Marked box to mark a selected group of records (for any reason) and then use the Find Marked Records button to see them in a list.
