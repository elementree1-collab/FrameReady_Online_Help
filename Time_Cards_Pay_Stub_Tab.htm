Pay Stub Tab Explained

  • Regular, Overtime and Vacation pay is automatically calculated based on hours worked and pay rates entered in the Info Tab.

  • Up to seven deductions can be entered.

  • Total Deductions value is calculated automatically.

  • The employee's Net Pay is calculated automatically (Gross Pay - Deductions).

Tip: If you change any of the data in any of these tabs, then it will be changed it in all Time Cards (old and new) for that Employee.
