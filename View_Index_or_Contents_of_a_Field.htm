View Index or Contents of a Field

Anywhere in FrameReady

You can view a list of all the items ever entered into a field by clicking two keys on your keyboard.

  • You do not have to be in the Find mode to perform this function.

How to View a Field Index

One of the benefits of using the index is for items which you are not sure of the spelling. Or if you want to perform a find for something but are not sure how it was entered previously.

  1. On any screen in FrameReady, click into a field.

  2. In Windows, press Control + I (the letter "i")
    In macOS, press Command + I (the letter "i")

  3. An index of all previous used listings appears (in alphabetical order).

  4. Click the word you want to enter into the field, then click the Paste button.

    Or click Cancel.
