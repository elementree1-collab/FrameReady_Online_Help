Products Overview

Main Menu > Products

  • The Products file is where your retail merchandise is entered and stored, including art of any type, ready-made frames, gifts and crafts, and any other stock item that has a set retail price.

  • Your framing components are stored in the Price Codes file.

  • Each product item has a stock number that can be used for automatic lookup when filling out an Invoice.

Note: sheets of matboard and lengths of moulding may be sold on an Invoice but they are not stored in this file. Instead, use their Retail Pricing tab to set a retail price.

  • The Products file also tracks inventory, date of last purchase, and reorder levels.
