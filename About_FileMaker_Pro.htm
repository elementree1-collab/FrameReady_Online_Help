About FileMaker Pro

FileMaker is the application used to design, develop, and deploy FrameReady

  1. FileMaker is bundled with FrameReady as per a contractual agreement between Claris International Inc (formerly FileMaker, Inc) and Adatasol Inc.

  2. For networking purposes, FileMaker must also be installed on each additional computer or FileMaker Go on each additional iPad.

  3. A network license fee for each device (computer and/or iPad) must be paid to Adatasol Inc.

  4. Users with two (2) or more network licenses must upgrade to FileMaker Server.

The agreement between Claris (formerly FileMaker Inc.) and Adatasol Inc. clearly states that we shall provide all technical support for the FileMaker application. It also states that each copy bundled with FrameReady is licensed to Adatasol Inc and is not to be regarded a separate item. In other words, your copy/ copies of FileMaker are registered with Adatasol Inc and not with Claris International (formerly FileMaker Inc.).

FrameReady is an SBA (Solution Bundle Agreement) member of Claris International Inc. This means that we have passed the certification to have FileMaker Software bundled with FrameReady when we sell it. As part of this agreement with Claris, this also means that the copy of FileMaker you receive with your purchase of FrameReady cannot be used to create other programs. It is restricted to be used solely with FrameReady. Our licensing with Claris is in large volume licenses.
