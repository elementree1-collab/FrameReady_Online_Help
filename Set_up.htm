Set up your Operations Info

Main Menu > Setup Data > Operations tab

Use this section to enter information pertaining to operational areas of your business.

Setup Data - Operations tab Explained

Operations

Business Days Checkboxes

  • The days your business is open. An X in the check box indicates the days your shop is open for production.

  • The purpose of this feature is to prevent a Work Order due date from falling on a day  you are closed. This feature is optional, e.g., if you would like all due dates to fall on Saturday, then put an X in that box only.

Business Hours Field

  • Your business hours, for example:  Mon-Thu 10-5, Fri 10-8, Sat 10-2 

Calculations Checkbox

  • (Read only). FrameReady makes all calculations in metric, when checked. Unchecked indicates imperial measurements.

Paper Size Dropdown

  • Preferred Paper Size for printing, Letter or Legal.

Options for calculating Work Order due date

  • See: Set up Work Order Due Date Options

Add Excluded Date Button

  • Click to reveal the Add Excluded Date feature.

Excluded Dates Field

  • If a calculated Due Date falls on a listed date, then FrameReady advances the due date by one day.

Description Field

  • Description of the Excluded Date (see above).

Add Excluded Date Button

  • Click to create a new Excluded Date line item/entry.

How to set up Operations Information

  1. In the "Business Days" section, select the days your business is open.

    An X in the check box indicates the days your shop is open for production.

    Unchecked days will not be used for any Work Order Due Date.

  2. Enter your Business Hours.

    For example:  Mon-Thu 10-5, Fri 10-8, Sat 10-2 

  3. Chose your preferred Paper Size for printing, Letter or Legal.

    If the Calculations in Metric box is checked, then your copy of FrameReady makes all calculations in metric.
